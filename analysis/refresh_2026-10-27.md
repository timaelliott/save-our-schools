## What changed

### pps.net
- **Scenarios page: unchanged.** Its text, links and buttons are the same as on Oct 25.
  - All 30 PDFs it links were downloaded again and are byte-identical to the archived copies: the overview, four regional summaries, 18 feeder-pattern charts, and the methodology and KPI documents with their translations.
  - The embedded Power BI dashboard is not archived as data.
- **New "What We Heard" recap (Oct 13–19):**
  - **Responses:** 1,501 survey responses, about 95% from communities affected by the scenarios [p.2](https://github.com/timaelliott/save-our-schools/blob/main/sources/pps_net/rightsizing/engagement/2026-10-13_to_19_engagement_recap_what_we_heard.pdf).
  - **Distrust of projections:** about one in five respondents questioned whether the enrollment projections are accurate [p.5](https://github.com/timaelliott/save-our-schools/blob/main/sources/pps_net/rightsizing/engagement/2026-10-13_to_19_engagement_recap_what_we_heard.pdf). The West region (Wells and Lincoln clusters) showed "the highest level of distrust toward district enrollment figures" and raised facility condition, seismic safety and the need for busing [p.6](https://github.com/timaelliott/save-our-schools/blob/main/sources/pps_net/rightsizing/engagement/2026-10-13_to_19_engagement_recap_what_we_heard.pdf).
  - **Responses by school, to date:** Sellwood MS 253, Cleveland HS 127, Stephenson 123, Duniway 112, Llewellyn 108, James John 106, Maplewood 102 (up from 83 a week earlier) [p.9–10](https://github.com/timaelliott/save-our-schools/blob/main/sources/pps_net/rightsizing/engagement/2026-10-13_to_19_engagement_recap_what_we_heard.pdf).
- **FAQ change:** one question was dropped, "How will teacher transfers, job changes, or layoffs be handled?" It had been paired with an answer about reusing closed buildings.
- **Calendar:** no event changes.
- **bond.pps.net:** unchanged.

### BoardBook
- **No new meetings** since Oct 25.
- **New minutes:**
  - **Oct 13 Intergovernmental Affairs Committee:** PAT and Community and Parents for Public Schools presented legislative priorities. Directors discussed raising or removing the special education funding cap, and Engelsman asked the Board to reach out to local elected officials on school funding.
  - **Oct 6 ethics training:** the minutes PDF has no extractable text.

They include the internal performance audit office overview, which plans to hire an auditor with bond-program expertise in 2026-27, plus the audit charter, the performance-auditing policy and background papers. None of this is about rightsizing.
- **No new Board videos.**
