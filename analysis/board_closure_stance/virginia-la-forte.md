## Bottom line

**Current score (Oct 11, 2026): 0. Range: −1 to +1.**

She has argued for consolidation "at scale" since March. On Sept 1 she said she was "wide open" on magnitude. By Oct 6 *(medium-confidence attribution)* she had tied her vote to two things:
- the November fiscal impact statement ("I feel like I can't make a decision without it");
- fixes for the North/Northeast "school deserts" that both scenarios create, including Peninsula and her own Sabin/Irvington area.

The most likely outcome is a **yes on an amended package that addresses those points**, and a **no, or support for deferral, on Scenario A as drafted**. KPTV reported on Oct 6 that she had not decided [NEWS:NEWS-20261006-kptv-2](https://www.kptv.com/2026/10/07/pps-board-member-parents-react-controversial-school-closure-plan/).

## Weak points

1. **The fiscal-impact condition has no stated pass mark.**

     - The Oregon PTA guidance gives the board research that closing a school "typically achieves only minimal operational savings if positions are simply redistributed" [BB:3991313 p.4](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/3991313?org=915#page=4).
     - PPS's SEGC evaluation says facility savings "are usually much smaller" because personnel dominates costs [BB:3997427 p.110](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/3997427?org=915#page=110).
     - Staff said PPS is "not um exclaiming some you know huge dollar amount from closures and savings" [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@3:44:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zOUH2Jq7Owo&t=13463s) *(speaker medium; summary attributes it to Armstrong)*.
   - The 11/17 statement is therefore likely to show modest net savings plus transition costs. Her own words do not say whether a small number, if transparent, would satisfy her.
   - Res. 7307, which she seconded, commits PPS to assess "financial impacts" in parallel. It does not contain her stronger 4/14 wording that one-time costs be "made public prior to Board action" [BB:3997273](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/3997273?org=915) [BB:3990653](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/3990653?org=915).
