## What this means for Maplewood (Analysis)

- **PPS still plans a Maplewood ISC move.** Staff described where Maplewood's K-2 ISC classroom would go if Maplewood closes: Markham, not Hayhurst. This matches the school profile, which shows the classroom moving to Markham under both scenarios [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/maplewood.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1JYIeqmstLm-IlaORhxbO1Wcz9z4Q4beC&usp=drive_copy). Nothing said at the meeting removes Maplewood from either scenario.
- **Hayhurst's room count.** Buno confirmed that Hayhurst already holds two SES classrooms, and this is why it can't take the ISC classroom [YT:V6r7UqxJ53s@1:35:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6r7UqxJ53s&t=5741s). The receiving-school math should treat those rooms as already in use.
- **Hayhurst's poverty figure.** A board member said on the record that Hayhurst's direct-certification figure in the deck is wrong (about 30%, not 14.8%) [YT:V6r7UqxJ53s@1:48:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6r7UqxJ53s&t=6482s). Buno answered "Yeah, we can look at that" [YT:V6r7UqxJ53s@1:48:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6r7UqxJ53s&t=6503s). If corrected, Hayhurst is a higher-need receiving school than the deck shows, which matters for the equity weighting of sending Maplewood students there.
- **Board signals.** Two of the three committee members said they want fewer closures:
  - Splitt: "a significantly smaller number of mergers" [YT:V6r7UqxJ53s@2:11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6r7UqxJ53s&t=7860s).
  - La Forte: "definitely not 14 and it's definitely not 11" [YT:V6r7UqxJ53s@3:24:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6r7UqxJ53s&t=12250s).
  - Armstrong's admission that closing schools "isn't a significant cost savings" [YT:V6r7UqxJ53s@3:03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6r7UqxJ53s&t=10990s) is the strongest staff statement yet supporting a smaller closure list.
  - Whether Maplewood stays on that list is decided in the superintendent's recommendation, which staff said will include the implementation plan and the special-education detail [YT:V6r7UqxJ53s@2:26:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6r7UqxJ53s&t=8763s) [YT:V6r7UqxJ53s@2:48:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6r7UqxJ53s&t=10117s).
