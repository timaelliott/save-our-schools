## How readiness and capacity were assessed (1:02:09–1:10:41)

**Franco** [YT:cyHU9UOQqvc@1:02:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cyHU9UOQqvc&t=3754s):
- Staff first screened sites by functional capacity, utilization and work orders, using TRIRIGA room data and floor-plan reviews with principals in June.
- After the scenarios were released, teams "did in-person walkthroughs with principals and other staff of 21 buildings", chosen as the sites with the largest increases.
- **Room sizes:** 24 seats for 500–800 sq ft, 27 for 800–1,000, and 30 for larger rooms.
- **What's excluded:** K-5/K-8 counts exclude the gym and one or two art/music rooms.
- **Special education rooms** are "set aside from the general instructional functional capacity", planned at 12 students.

**Chief Buno** [YT:cyHU9UOQqvc@1:06:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cyHU9UOQqvc&t=4012s):
- Focus classrooms also need a regulation space (SES programs) or a sensory space (ISC and CB programs).
- Related-service staff need workspace.
- Inclusive-schools sites need one flexible learning space each for K-2 and 3-5 [YT:cyHU9UOQqvc@1:09:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cyHU9UOQqvc&t=4162s).

## Seismic funds under Scenarios A and B (1:30:20–1:37:20)

**Eggers** [YT:cyHU9UOQqvc@1:30:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cyHU9UOQqvc&t=5443s):
- **Scenario A** moves the Rose City Park and Beach seismic money to receiving schools and high-risk elements:
  - Abernethy gym wall;
  - Arleta and Brentwood building parts;
  - Duniway;
  - Hosford cafeteria;
  - three load-bearing URM wall sections at Markham;
  - Rigler;
  - Rieke Building Parts A, B and C, with a state seismic grant application in progress for B and C;
  - Whitman.
- **Scenario B** moves the Beach money to Duniway and Rieke.
- **District-wide:** 45% of students are in building parts rated above 9.0, and 31–32% are on campuses with unreinforced masonry, based on 2025 values. The share falls under each scenario [YT:cyHU9UOQqvc@1:35:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cyHU9UOQqvc&t=5708s).

## Analysis: what this meeting means for the Maplewood/Hayhurst case

- **Hayhurst's corrected historic peak still includes Odyssey.** The 2015-16 count of 499 combined Hayhurst's neighborhood program (277) and Odyssey (222) [PPS:pps_net/data-archives/2021-22/enrollment_history_detail_2021-22.pdf p.1](https://www.pps.net/fs/resource-manager/view/3a0df1e6-39a6-4b6a-8c85-9b0ceb06bd4d#page=1) [PPS:pps_net/boundaries_2016/wayback_2017/2015-16_School_Profiles.pdf p.212](https://github.com/timaelliott/save-our-schools/blob/main/sources/pps_net/boundaries_2016/wayback_2017/2015-16_School_Profiles.pdf).
  - Hayhurst's own K-5 program peaked at 396 in 2019-20, which is also the 2019-20 figure in PPS's utilization model [PPS:pps_net/data-archives/2021-22/enrollment_history_detail_2021-22.pdf p.1](https://www.pps.net/fs/resource-manager/view/3a0df1e6-39a6-4b6a-8c85-9b0ceb06bd4d#page=1) [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/utilization_model/static_v1_public_utilization_model_2026-11-02.xlsx#DISTRICT UTILIZATION v1!R40].
  - The projected 2027 peak of 469 [BB:14644366 p.19](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14644366?org=915#page=19) is therefore about 18% above anything Hayhurst's K-5 program has held. It is close to the 2015-16 combined count, a level PPS's 2016 profile flagged as density above 20, meaning "little or no room left for growth" [PPS:pps_net/boundaries_2016/wayback_2017/2015-16_School_Profiles.pdf p.318](https://github.com/timaelliott/save-our-schools/blob/main/sources/pps_net/boundaries_2016/wayback_2017/2015-16_School_Profiles.pdf).
- **Two of the three schools taking Maplewood students were flagged.** Markham, which receives Maplewood's Intensive Skills class, was rated "not yet ready" for transportation. Rieke wasn't one of the 21 walked sites, but its gym and other building parts are on the Scenario A seismic list.
- **Staff's account of classroom counts helps explain the 22-vs-23 question** (see `analysis/topics/hayhurst_capacity_2016_vs_2026.md`). Evenson said the LRFP counts the gym as a classroom and that walkthrough counts leave out smaller flex rooms. That fits a building with 22 classrooms plus a gym.
- **The decision timeline.** Staff said space plans for special education had not been published before the scenarios. Most sites beyond the 21 will be walked only after the Nov 17 recommendation, and receiving-site special-education facility reviews are scheduled for March [BB:14654773 p.28](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14654773?org=915#page=28). Both are after the materials the board will use to decide.
