## Findings for the Southwest (Maplewood) package

### 1. The "Maplewood, Rieke, Hayhurst and Markham" profile shows Markham's Scenario A numbers only
The p.19 header reads "519 Enrollment, 45 SpEd Students (9%), 25.7% Direct Certification" [BB:14654773 p.19](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14654773?org=915#page=19).
- 519 is Markham's Scenario A 2027-28 projection, and 25.7% is Markham's Scenario A poverty rate in the Markham family guide (`analysis/data/school_impact.csv`).
- Markham's growth in Scenario A comes mostly from Stephenson (70%), not Maplewood. Maplewood's students go 70% to Hayhurst and 30% to Rieke (school_impact.csv).
- Neither receiving school's numbers appear in the header:
  - Hayhurst: 472 projected, 20.8% special ed.
  - Rieke: 348 rising to 434, 16.1% special ed.
- The same method checks out on other profiles. Sitton (464, 57.2%), Whitman (437, 56.1%), MLK Jr (376, 37.0%), Duniway (544, 10.8%) and Glencoe (318, 17.7%) are each the receiving school's Scenario A 2027-28 projection and poverty rate.

### 2. The 9% special-ed figure is far below every other profile and PPS's own family guide
- Every other group profile shows 16–34% special ed [BB:14654773 pp.13–23](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14654773?org=915).
- The Markham family guide gives Markham 15.1% special ed in Scenario A, about 78 of 519 students (school_impact.csv).
- Today the four schools serve 202 students with IEPs: Maplewood 48, Rieke 38, Hayhurst 67, Markham 49 [BB:14654773 pp.24–25](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14654773?org=915).

### 3. The profile's caseload counts 59 students and leaves out Maplewood's Learning Center and speech students
- Caseload listed [BB:14654773 p.19](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14654773?org=915#page=19):
  - "38 Students - Rieke LC"
  - "12 Students - ISC Markham"
  - "9 Students - Hayhurst SES"
- Students served today [BB:14654773 pp.50, 51, 53](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14654773?org=915):
  - Maplewood: 30 Learning Center, 11 speech-only, 7 K-2 ISC.
  - Hayhurst: 36 Learning Center, 16 speech-only, 15 SES.
  - Markham: 24 Learning Center, 11 speech-only, 10 ISC.
- Analysis:
  - "38" is exactly Rieke's current total IEP count [BB:14654773 p.25](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14654773?org=915#page=25).
  - None of Maplewood's 30 Learning Center or 11 speech-only students appears anywhere in the future profile. The profile also has no speech-only line, which every other group profile has.
  - By the deck's own ratio (0.5 FTE : 15), Maplewood's 30 LC students alone need 1.0 FTE.
- Hayhurst's SES caseload falls from 15 students [BB:14654773 p.50](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14654773?org=915#page=50) to 9 [BB:14654773 p.19](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14654773?org=915#page=19) with the same 2.0 FTE. The deck doesn't explain why; SES is a regional placement, not a boundary one [BB:14654773 p.10](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14654773?org=915#page=10).

### 4. Support staffing is less than half of what the three profiled schools have now
- The future profile lists 0.8 FTE psychologist, 12 paraprofessionals, 1.0 QMHP and 0.8 FTE SLP [BB:14654773 p.19](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14654773?org=915#page=19).
- Today, Maplewood, Hayhurst and Markham alone have 1.8 FTE psych, 20 paras and 1.8 FTE SLP [BB:14654773 pp.50, 51, 53](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14654773?org=915). Rieke has no current-state profile in the deck.
- Analysis: if p.19 is meant as the receiving schools' total, it cuts support staff while adding students. If it only covers the focus programs, then the Learning Center and speech services for about 130 students at Maplewood, Hayhurst and Markham aren't described anywhere.

### 5. Markham needs a second ISC room that the utilization model doesn't count, which puts it over 100%
- The future profile gives Markham "2.0 FTE ISC Classrooms" and "2 ISC Classrooms (Markham)" [BB:14654773 p.19](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14654773?org=915#page=19).
- PPS's utilization model counts one SPED/DHH focus room at Markham [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/utilization_model/static_v1_public_utilization_model_2026-11-02.xlsx#DISTRICT UTILIZATION v1!R62].
- Analysis, using the model's own formula (stations reduced = 27 × room reductions − 15 × SPED rooms; Markham's 0.95 Title I factor):
  - Base capacity: 738 − (27×7 − 15×2) = 579.
  - Functional capacity: 579 × 0.95 = 550, down from 561.
  - Markham's Scenario A peak of 554 (2030-31) is then **101%**.

### 6. Hayhurst's special-ed room count matches PPS's 525, which weakens our ≈97% correction
- The deck lists Hayhurst's special-ed needs as a Learning Center, K-2 SES, 3-5 SES, de-escalation, SLP and psych [BB:14654773 p.50](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14654773?org=915#page=50). The future profile lists "2 SES Classrooms, Sensory (Hayhurst)" [BB:14654773 p.19](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14654773?org=915#page=19).
- The utilization model sets aside 2 SPED rooms and 1 LC at Hayhurst, so the deck agrees with the 525 figure [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/utilization_model/static_v1_public_utilization_model_2026-11-02.xlsx#DISTRICT UTILIZATION v1!R40].
- Other sources point higher:
  - The Nov 9 FIO profile says 7 SPED classrooms and "4 Classrooms Total" [BB:14644366 p.19](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14644366?org=915#page=19).
  - Director Splitt said SCES "takes up four classrooms" [YT:9kX3vnfRlvY@4:01:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9kX3vnfRlvY&t=14512s) (confidence: high, per the existing Nov 4 summary).
- Analysis: PPS's documents disagree. The ≈97% figure on `analysis/topics/maplewood_claims_check.html` rests on the FIO deck and Splitt. This deck supports PPS's 90%, unless the de-escalation and sensory spaces take full classrooms.
