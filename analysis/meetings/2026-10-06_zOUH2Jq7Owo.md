## Summary
- **The first public board session on the closure scenarios, held in front of a crowd of about 500.**
  - The scenarios were released the night before [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@2:51:34].
  - "Save our school" chants delayed the start. The chair repeatedly threatened to move the meeting and promised a larger venue [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@0:00:00–0:02:43].
  - The board recessed for 5 minutes at 1:27, and again at 2:18 after repeated interruptions, before deciding to stay [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@1:27:12] [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@2:18:34–2:21:06].
- **The two scenarios** [BB:14509719 p.1–3]:
  - **Scenario A:** 14 closures: Beach, Buckman, Creston, Irvington, James John, Lewis, Maplewood, Marysville, Peninsula, Rose City Park, Sabin, Sellwood MS, Stephenson, Woodmere. Also 38 boundary adjustments, 13 program moves, and K-8→K-5/6-8 reconfigurations [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@1:51:19–1:55:22].
  - **Scenario B:** 11 closures. Lewis, Stephenson and Rose City Park stay open [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@1:55:22].
  - Spanish DLI consolidates into four regional hubs: Chávez (N), Rigler (NE), Lent (SE), Ainsworth (W). MLC's high school ends and MLC becomes a K-8. Odyssey moves to MLC [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@1:52:41–1:55:22].
  - The district goes from 82 schools to 68 under A [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@1:58:48].
- **The headline KPI (Calvert).**
  - Students in schools above the enrollment threshold rise from 48% (status quo) to **80%** under A by 2031-32 [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@1:48:34]. For students in poverty the rise is 42%→81%.
  - Schools above threshold rise to about two-thirds (67%) under A and almost 60% under B; B leaves 43% of schools below [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@1:47:11] [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@1:49:57] [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@1:56:02].
  - Calvert: the magnitude was chosen for "some stability for at least 5 years" [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@2:15:11].
- **Thresholds are "a bridge" (Franco).** The thresholds are 360 (K-5), 500 (K-8/MS) and 1,000 (HS). Franco said they are "not what we would say is ideal state" and are grounded in SEGC lessons and the QEM [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@1:37:33]. Receiving schools are capped at two per closure [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@1:39:34] [BB:14511742 p.11].
- **Per-student cost (Armstrong).**
  - General-fund non-equity cost per student: K-5 $10,037 versus MS $9,281 [BB:14511742 p.6]. Armstrong said "for the last eight years, we have taken from some schools to subsidize others" [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@1:31:23].
  - Staff explained elementary costs as baseline minimums, such as a full-time counselor in every K-5 regardless of size [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@3:56:33].
- **Who moves.** 27% of K-8 students move under A (7,981) and 24% under B (6,988) [BB:14511742 p.44].
  - Rates are similar across groups: poverty 27%, special education 27%, multilingual learners 31%, historically underserved by race 28%.
  - Smith: "we are not asking them to carry the heaviest load" [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@2:49:31–2:50:54].

## Rightsizing / closures
**Staff rationale, school by school (Smith; Calvert for regions)** [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@2:23:07–2:48:08]:
- **West:**
  - Maplewood → Hayhurst and Rieke.
  - Stephenson → Markham and Capitol Hill (A only).
  - No major facility issues cited for either; the choice was geography and student density.
  - Maplewood's middle-school feed moves from Jackson to Gray. Skyline goes to Roosevelt.
- **North:**
  - Spanish DLI consolidates at Chávez K-5, and the neighborhood program moves to Rosa Parks.
  - James John → Sitton (ADA).
  - Beach → Chief Joseph.
  - Peninsula → Rosa Parks ("modernized facility").
  - Sabin → MLK Jr. (Sabin about 200 students). MLK's Mandarin moves to Boise-Eliot/Humboldt.
  - K-8 middle grades move: Faubion → Ockley Green, Vernon → Tubman, Chávez → George. Astor stays a K-8 because George would be near capacity.
- **Grant-McDaniel:**
  - Irvington (223 projected) → Beverly Cleary.
  - **Rose City Park closes for facility reasons despite enrollment above 360**: "the facilities were what drove that decision for scenario A" [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@2:33:55]. Vietnamese DLI moves to Vestal.
  - Clark Mandarin (about six kindergartners) moves to Woodstock.
  - Laurelhurst middle grades move to Mt. Tabor; Beverly Cleary 6-8 to Beaumont.
- **Cleveland-Franklin (six closures in A):**
  - Buckman (facility condition) → Abernathy and Sunnyside.
  - Creston (187 projected) → Atkinson; its deaf and hard-of-hearing program goes to Glencoe.
  - Lewis → Duniway (A only).
  - Marysville → Arleta. Marysville has more students, but Arleta has the footprint. The move gets **one extra year** so former Lent students are not moved twice [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@2:42:43].
  - Woodmere → Whitman.
  - **Sellwood MS closes in fall 2028**, split to Hosford and Brentwood, on facility grounds: steam leaks, water intrusion, no ADA gym access [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@2:45:25–2:46:47].
  - Bridger and Sunnyside become K-5. Sunnyside loses its focus-option lottery.
