## Summary
- **Armstrong reframed closures as a budget-independent decision, but put numbers on what they buy.**
  - "Closing school buildings is not about reducing the deficit" [YT:xByUlijpcnE@1:07:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=4076s).
  - She described a projected **$65M General Fund reduction for 2027-28** [YT:xByUlijpcnE@1:10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=4211s). About **$44M (68%) of it is "increased net pension costs"** [YT:xByUlijpcnE@1:11:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=4308s).
  - Without closures, the school share of the cuts equals **about 223 FTE** [YT:xByUlijpcnE@1:14:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=4473s).
  - **Scenario A** (14 closures) saves about **$14M** in operations, "approximately 97 full-time positions" preserved [YT:xByUlijpcnE@1:14:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=4499s) [YT:xByUlijpcnE@1:15:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=4514s). **Scenario B** (11) saves about **$11M**, or 76 FTE [YT:xByUlijpcnE@1:15:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=4520s).
  - She called these "planning estimates, not final staffing decisions or guaranteed reinvestments" [YT:xByUlijpcnE@1:15:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=4539s).
  - Analysis: these are the first per-scenario savings figures stated publicly by PPS in the archive. They come from the superintendent's slides, which are not in the BoardBook packet.
- **Christy Splitt announced her own smaller plan.**
  - It targets the 13 schools forecast under 250 students for next fall and closes or merges **6 buildings** [YT:xByUlijpcnE@4:56:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=17819s) [YT:xByUlijpcnE@5:02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=18145s).
  - It keeps the middle-school consolidation [YT:xByUlijpcnE@4:59:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=17954s) and lets Sunnyside and Bridger middle-grade students move as a group [YT:xByUlijpcnE@4:59:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=17998s) [YT:xByUlijpcnE@5:01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=18067s).
  - She called it "a beginning of a phased approach" [YT:xByUlijpcnE@5:01:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=18106s).
  - She said the Oregonian had already summarized it [YT:xByUlijpcnE@4:57:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=17877s).
  - Public commenters earlier referred to a "Scenario D" raised that afternoon [YT:xByUlijpcnE@1:44:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=6287s) [YT:xByUlijpcnE@1:46:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=6400s). The Oregonian's story that afternoon gives the plan's contents from her memo: close Creston, Buckman, Woodmere, Sabin, Irvington and Peninsula; merge Creston with Arleta rather than Atkinson; keep Spanish DLI co-located for now; leave Stephenson and Maplewood open while a Southwest boundary process runs [NEWS:NEWS-20261020-oregonian-1](https://www.oregonlive.com/education/2026/10/2-portland-public-schools-board-members-pitch-alternatives-to-closing-14-schools.html). The memo itself is not in the archive.
