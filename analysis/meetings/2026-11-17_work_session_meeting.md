## Financial update (1:22–2:02)

- **The gap.** The $65M gap "is a persistent number". Two-thirds of it is higher PERS employer rates as PPS's pension-bond side accounts expire. Without that, PPS "would be just over $20 million of a gap" [YT:hbSByH9nzfA@1:33:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hbSByH9nzfA&t=5592s).
- **Pension rates.** The combined pension rate spikes almost 10% from 2026-27 to 2027-28. Each percentage point is about $4.5M. PPS has no reserves to bridge the spike [YT:hbSByH9nzfA@1:34:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hbSByH9nzfA&t=5662s).
- **Rightsizing and the budget.** "PPS is not using uh right-sizing is a method of closing the budget gap. We have to close that gap whether there are any changes or not" [YT:hbSByH9nzfA@1:36:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hbSByH9nzfA&t=5809s). Armstrong added that closures would make little difference to the deficit, because freed staff positions (FTE) are reinvested [YT:hbSByH9nzfA@1:55:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hbSByH9nzfA&t=6958s). She also said PPS has cut about $200M over six years: "So we are at an emergency state" [YT:hbSByH9nzfA@1:41:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hbSByH9nzfA&t=6068s).
- **Reserves.** The unaudited 2025-26 fund balance is 4–5%. La Forte asked whether any of the contingency had been transferred out of reserves. Morrison said the only reserve used was the PERS reserve, and that the 2025-26 shortfall shows up as a lower beginning fund balance [YT:hbSByH9nzfA@1:42:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hbSByH9nzfA&t=6120s). Armstrong said reserves would be back at 5% by year-end [YT:hbSByH9nzfA@2:01:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hbSByH9nzfA&t=7282s).
- **Reductions list.** The list of reductions comes in January [YT:hbSByH9nzfA@1:54:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hbSByH9nzfA&t=6867s).
- **Reporting.** Engelsman and La Forte asked for the quarterly financial reports required by Board Policy 8.20.010 [YT:hbSByH9nzfA@1:46:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hbSByH9nzfA&t=6398s) [YT:hbSByH9nzfA@1:58:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hbSByH9nzfA&t=7130s).
