## F. Rightsizing Methodology [BB:14695410](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695410?org=915)

The methodology names the model's inputs [BB:14695410 p.2](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695410?org=915#page=2):
- PSU projections, with 2025-26 as the base year;
- scenario configurations;
- "3-Year Historical Averages" of where students from each school redistribute;
- facility capacity from the District Utilization model.

**Criteria.** It lists nine criteria [BB:14695410 p.4](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695410?org=915#page=4) [BB:14695410 p.5](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695410?org=915#page=5):
1. One same-language DLI program per region.
2. Sustainable enrollment by 2031-32: 360 students for elementary schools, 500 for middle and K-8 schools, and 1,100 for high schools.
3. No more than two receiving schools per closure.
4. Minimal boundary changes.
5. Facility improvements.
6. Compact boundaries (students within 1.0 mile of an elementary school, 1.5 miles of a middle school).
7. Building use between 70% and 100%.
8. Demographics.
9. Fewer split feeders.

The criteria "were weighed against one another rather than applied as a fixed ranking" [BB:14695410 p.1](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695410?org=915#page=1).

**Assumptions.** Both scenarios assume "the PSU projection of students will be enrolled in PPS during all years." Implementation runs from 2027-28 through the start of 2028-29 [BB:14695410 p.6](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695410?org=915#page=6).

**Analysis.** The document gives no rule that explains why Maplewood, Stephenson, James John and Sellwood MS came off the list. The memo's own reasons are in the main analysis:
- Maplewood's enrollment passed 300 this year, and community members raised concerns about nearby housing development.
- Stephenson's enrollment has leveled off at about 285, with a two-years-of-decline review trigger [BB:14695483 p.27](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695483?org=915#page=27).

Under the 360 threshold, Maplewood (PSU's status-quo projection for 2031-32 is 247) is still below target. The memo lists it among four schools under 260 to "monitor closely" [BB:14695483 p.43](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695483?org=915#page=43).

## P. Rightsizing KPIs [BB:14695419](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695419?org=915)

This attachment defines the indicators and gives no values:
- **Opportunity and stability:**
  - % of schools above the threshold;
  - % of focal students in schools above it;
  - enrollment range.
- **Physical environment and pathways:**
  - % of schools at 70–100% use;
  - % of students within 1.0 / 1.5 miles;
  - number of split feeders;
  - % of middle-school students going to the same high school.
- **Balance and impact:**
  - % of focal students moved;
  - Title I and CSI/TSI schools affected;
  - number of students moved [BB:14695419 p.3](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695419?org=915#page=3) [BB:14695419 p.4](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695419?org=915#page=4).

**Facility conditions.** Building condition "will not be the primary driver of scenario selection," and "no single facility measure will be the primary determining factor" [BB:14695419 p.5](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695419?org=915#page=5).

**Analysis.** Several closure reports name building condition as the deciding factor between paired schools: Buckman, Rose City Park, Beach and Peninsula [BB:14695445 p.136](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695445?org=915#page=136) [BB:14695445 p.139](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695445?org=915#page=139) [BB:14695445 p.154](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695445?org=915#page=154) [BB:14695445 p.157](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695445?org=915#page=157). That fits this rule only if condition decided which school in a pair stays open, not whether a pair is consolidated at all.

## Southwest staffing in the memo's 2027-28 model

The memo models staffing for 2027-28 [BB:14695483 p.53](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695483?org=915#page=53) [BB:14695483 p.54](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695483?org=915#page=54). The FTE excludes special education, multilingual, Title I and Measure 98 positions. "Class size" is PPS's weighted average including equity FTE.

| School | 2026-27 FTE | 2027-28 FTE (status quo = recommendation) | Change | Class size 2026-27 → 2027-28 | Projected enrollment 2027-28 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maplewood | 18.65 | 16.35 | −2.30 | 25.0 → 23.2 | 278 |
| Hayhurst | 18.65 | 15.85 | −2.80 | 22.4 → 23.8 | 274 |
| Rieke | 16.95 | 15.35 | −1.60 | 23.2 → 22.9 | 252 |
| Markham | 21.25 | 17.55 | −3.70 | 24.1 → 24.0 | 312 |
| Capitol Hill | 18.15 | 16.55 | −1.60 | 26.7 → 25.8 | 310 |
| Stephenson | 16.95 | 14.15 | −2.80 | 24.5 → 27.1 | 271 |

**How the model works.** It applies "identical baseline reduction criteria across the district":
- 277.5 FTE gross reductions under the status quo and 308.5 under the recommendation, against a target of 223 FTE;
- "Class size ratios were not adjusted" in either model;
- 40.4 FTE of the cut is tied to projected enrollment decline [BB:14695483 p.50](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695483?org=915#page=50) [BB:14695483 p.51](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695483?org=915#page=51).

The receiving-school reinvestment pool of 15–20 FTE goes to 19 schools. None of them is in the Southwest [BB:14695483 p.54](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695483?org=915#page=54).

**Analysis.**
- Maplewood and Hayhurst both lose more than 2 FTE in the model whether or not rightsizing passes. These are budget and enrollment reductions, not rightsizing ones.
- Maplewood's cut follows a projected 2027-28 enrollment of 278, below its September 2026 count of 303 (`analysis/data/enrollment_2026-27_sept_by_school.csv`).
- PPS calls these "preliminary planning estimates" to be updated after PSU's January forecasts [BB:14695483 p.50](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695483?org=915#page=50). Whether PSU's January forecast holds Maplewood near 300 or near 278 is therefore worth about one homeroom teacher.
