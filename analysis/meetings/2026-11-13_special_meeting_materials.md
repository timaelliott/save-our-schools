This is the only item on the agenda. It is not a rightsizing item, and nothing in the packet mentions school closures or any Southwest school.

## What the resolution says

- The recitals say the Board "has reviewed a news report alleging that some PPS employees have used the Signal messaging application to communicate about PPS business", and that Signal "is not a PPS-approved platform for conducting official PPS communications" [BB:14678231 p.1](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14678231?org=915#page=1).
- It notes that using non-approved apps "for some purposes would not necessarily violate PPS policy or state law" [BB:14678231 p.1](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14678231?org=915#page=1).
- It directs the General Counsel to work with outside counsel on fact-finding and report back to the Board. The scope [BB:14678231 p.1](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14678231?org=915#page=1):
  1. whether Signal or similar apps are used by "PPS management employees" for PPS matters, and for what topics and purposes;
  2. whether employees kept information sent through such apps and whether it can be collected and preserved under document-retention standards;
  3. other compliance matters arising from what is found, or that could inform policy changes.

## Context in the archive

- The only earlier item in the archive about Signal use is an opinion newsletter of Oct 25, 2026. It published a photo, said to come from a PFSP press release, that allegedly shows a Signal exchange involving Chief of Staff Deborah Kafoury during the Oct 20 work session. The newsletter labels the photo and attributions unverified [NEWS:NEWS-20261025-recalibrate-1].
- The resolution does not name the news report it refers to, or any employee.

Analysis: The packet does not touch rightsizing. It matters to the closure process only indirectly: messages about PPS business kept on Signal could be public records, including any about rightsizing. Watch the Nov 13 video for the vote and for any discussion of which topics the messages covered.

What the clean amendment would change, compared with the posted resolution:

- **Who runs it.** Instead of the General Counsel working with outside counsel, the Board would direct the District "to immediately retain independent legal counsel to advise and represent the Board", and General Counsel and staff "will not have authority to direct independent counsel or the fact-finding process" [BB:14686893 p.1].
- **Whom it covers.** The recitals name a "Willamette Week news report alleging that the Superintendent and Portland Public Schools (“District”) “leaders” used the Signal messaging application", and images of Signal conversations "made publicly available online" [BB:14686893 p.1]. The scope covers the Superintendent, "current and/or former District leaders", and entities acting for the District [BB:14686893 p.1]. The posted version referred only to "some PPS employees" and "PPS management employees" [BB:14678231 p.1].
- **Scope.** Four items: use of messaging platforms (who, when, why); whether leaders "directed employees to use non-PPS-approved messaging platforms"; records preservation and recovery; and compliance with statutes, policy and records-retention law [BB:14686893 p.2].
- **New duties.** The District must "immediately preserve all potentially relevant records", and independent counsel must set up "a confidential channel" for current and former employees to provide information [BB:14686893 p.2].
- **Deadline.** Findings would go "directly and solely to the full Board by December 11, 2026", or an interim report by that date if more time is needed [BB:14686893 p.1–2].
- The amendment drops the posted recital that some uses of non-approved apps "would not necessarily violate PPS policy or state law" [BB:14678231 p.1]; it is absent from the clean text [BB:14686893 p.1]. (The markup's extracted text does not show strike-through, so read the PDF for the redline itself [BB:14686894 p.1–2].)

Analysis: Still not a rightsizing item. If the amendment passes, the Board would receive findings on December 11, after the expected superintendent recommendation and probably before a closure vote. Any Signal messages about rightsizing could then come up in that window. Nothing in either document mentions schools, closures or the Southwest package.
