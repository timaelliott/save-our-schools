# Master timeline: PPS bond, budget, enrollment and rightsizing (Jan 2023 – Oct 2026)

Compiled 2026-10-11. Each line reads: date — event — citations. Citation formats follow `CLAUDE.md`.

How this file was built:
- It merges Board minutes and votes, staff presentations (BoardBook), on-the-record statements (video), news reports and data releases.
- News-only items are marked "(news)". Check them against primary sources before relying on them.
- **Bold** marks the 30 events most important for understanding the December 15, 2026 closure decision.
- Related files: `timeline_from_news.md` has more news detail, `meetings/` has per-meeting notes, and `topics/rightsizing.md` §3.1 lists process milestones only.

Corrections from `_verification_report.md` are applied. In particular:
- the Res. 7229 roll call is on p.4;
- the May 26 and June 23 votes come from the archived minutes;
- the 7296 guardrail amendment failed 3–4.
