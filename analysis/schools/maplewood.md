## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Building closes; 70% of boundary to Hayhurst, 30% to Rieke; middle grades to Robert Gray instead of Jackson; K-2 Intensive Skills Classroom moves to Markham.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/maplewood.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1JYIeqmstLm-IlaORhxbO1Wcz9z4Q4beC&usp=drive_copy):
> Maplewood School would close as a school building. 70% of the neighborhood boundary would move to Hayhurst K-5, and 30% would move to Rieke K-5. Middle grade students would move to Robert Gray Middle School instead of Jackson. The K-2 Intensive Skills Classroom would move to Markham K-5 to rejoin the 3-5 classroom.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/maplewood.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1JYIeqmstLm-IlaORhxbO1Wcz9z4Q4beC&usp=drive_copy):
> Low Expected enrollment: Maplewood’s enrollment was expected to drop to 247 students against a building space of 465. Nearby Hayhurst and Rieke have available space to welcome these families.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/maplewood.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1JYIeqmstLm-IlaORhxbO1Wcz9z4Q4beC&usp=drive_copy):
> Geographic split: 70% of students would move to Hayhurst because most of the Maplewood area is closer to Hayhurst. As we look at who bears the burden of moving, we wanted to reduce the impact on students experiencing poverty and underserved communities and that is a contributing factor for moving Maplewood to Hayhurst.

**Board-member alternatives and advocacy (Oct 20, 2026):**
- Splitt's alternative "Scenario D" (memo reported Oct 20) would keep this school open for now [NEWS:NEWS-20261020-oregonian-1](https://www.oregonlive.com/education/2026/10/2-portland-public-schools-board-members-pitch-alternatives-to-closing-14-schools.html).
- Scenario D would instead start a Southwest boundary process among eight elementaries (Ainsworth, Bridlemile, Rieke, Hayhurst, Maplewood, Capitol Hill, Markham, Stephenson) [NEWS:NEWS-20261020-oregonian-1](https://www.oregonlive.com/education/2026/10/2-portland-public-schools-board-members-pitch-alternatives-to-closing-14-schools.html). Safe Structures PPS lists Maplewood among proposed closures with recent seismic investment [NEWS:NEWS-20261020-wweek-1](https://www.wweek.com/news/schools/2026/10/20/parent-group-asks-pps-for-seismic-bond-spending-plan-amid-rightsizing/).

## 2016 boundary change (Resolution 5256)

- **To Rieke:** "the area located south of SW Caldew St. and east of SW 25th Ave". The resolution calls this "Additional overcrowding relief for Maplewood school" and says the area is less than a mile from Rieke, so bused students could walk or bike instead [PPS:pps_net/boundaries_2016/2016-05-17_board_packet.pdf p.21](https://github.com/timaelliott/save-our-schools/blob/main/sources/pps_net/boundaries_2016/2016-05-17_board_packet.pdf).
- The same resolution moved Maplewood's middle-school feeder from Gray to Jackson beginning in 2017 [PPS:pps_net/boundaries_2016/2016-05-17_board_packet.pdf p.20](https://github.com/timaelliott/save-our-schools/blob/main/sources/pps_net/boundaries_2016/2016-05-17_board_packet.pdf).

PPS's 2016 tables count 48 Maplewood-neighborhood students reassigned out and none in [PPS:pps_net/boundaries_2016/dbrac/Quadrant_KPIs_SuptRecommendationsWest.pdf p.3](https://github.com/timaelliott/save-our-schools/blob/main/sources/pps_net/boundaries_2016/dbrac/Quadrant_KPIs_SuptRecommendationsWest.pdf). Analysis: balancing that table puts about 34 of them at Hayhurst and about 14 at Rieke. See [2016_boundary_changes.md](../topics/2016_boundary_changes.md#how-many-students-maplewood-lost-and-where-they-went).

PPS's family summary of the change has a map labeling the "Maplewood to Hayhurst" and "Maplewood to Rieke" areas [PPS:pps_net/boundaries_2016/2016_final_west_side_boundary_changes.pdf p.2–3].

Analysis: in 2016 PPS gave Maplewood overcrowding as the reason for shrinking its zone. In 2026, low enrollment is part of the case for closing it.
