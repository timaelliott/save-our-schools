## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Building closes (structural limits, multi-story accessibility); neighborhood K-5 to Sitton (single-story); Spanish DLI to César Chávez.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/james_john.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1fEKbTKwDd1AQ5C21l30ntcX0x0BJ6WKq&usp=drive_copy):
> James John would close as a school building. Neighborhood K-5 students would move to Sitton K-5. Spanish Dual Language Immersion (DLI) students would move to César Chávez K-5.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/james_john.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1fEKbTKwDd1AQ5C21l30ntcX0x0BJ6WKq&usp=drive_copy):
> Building layout constraints: James John faces structural building limits and multi-story accessibility challenges. Accessible and special-focus centers: Bringing together neighborhood students into Sitton (a single-story school) and DLI students into César Chávez creates fully accessible, focused centers.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/james_john.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1fEKbTKwDd1AQ5C21l30ntcX0x0BJ6WKq&usp=drive_copy):
> Shared support services: Both James John and Sitton offer support for students with and without disabilities, SUN school and community support programs, and on-site food pantries.

**Board-member alternatives and advocacy (Oct 20, 2026):**
- Splitt's alternative "Scenario D" (memo reported Oct 20) would keep this school open for now [NEWS:NEWS-20261020-oregonian-1](https://www.oregonlive.com/education/2026/10/2-portland-public-schools-board-members-pitch-alternatives-to-closing-14-schools.html).
