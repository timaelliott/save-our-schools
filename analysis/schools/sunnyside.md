## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Changes to K-5 neighborhood school; no more lottery transfers (current lottery students may stay through 5th grade); welcomes 50% of Buckman (north of Burnside); 6-8 to Hosford; high school path changes from Franklin to Cleveland; receives Buckman SES classrooms.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/sunnyside.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1A9NQhIiZOvxn2EYFwrVIj_eVJqfNQgKx&usp=drive_copy):
> Sunnyside would change to a K-5 neighborhood school and would no longer take lottery transfers. Sunnyside would welcome 50% of the neighborhood school boundary from Buckman (the area north of Burnside). Middle grade (6th-8th) students would move to Hosford Middle School. Middle and high school path: High school students would move to Cleveland High School instead of Franklin High School.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/sunnyside.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1A9NQhIiZOvxn2EYFwrVIj_eVJqfNQgKx&usp=drive_copy):
> Richer middle school options: Changing Sunnyside from a K-8 special-focus choice school to a K-5 neighborhood school allows 6th-8th grade students to access a focused middle school with sustainable programming. This means students will have more access to educational activities. Better building conditions: Sunnyside provides a central location and better building conditions compared to the closing Buckman school.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/sunnyside.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1A9NQhIiZOvxn2EYFwrVIj_eVJqfNQgKx&usp=drive_copy):
> Current lottery protection: Current out-of-neighborhood students admitted through the lottery would be permitted to remain through 5th grade. Sunnyside shifts support balancing nearby elementary schools.
