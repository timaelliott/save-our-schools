## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open; Maplewood-area middle graders shift to Robert Gray; sibling preference for incoming 6th graders with older siblings at Jackson.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/jackson.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=15sjP_zPNFBjEJA1yD1Ocbi6fykU9Psmk&usp=drive_copy):
> Jackson Middle School would remain open as a neighborhood middle school. Jackson's boundary would be adjusted as middle grade students from the Maplewood area shift to Robert Gray Middle School.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/jackson.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=15sjP_zPNFBjEJA1yD1Ocbi6fykU9Psmk&usp=drive_copy):
> Balancing enrollment across Southwest: Moving Maplewood students to Robert Gray Middle School balances middle school space across the Wells cluster.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/jackson.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=15sjP_zPNFBjEJA1yD1Ocbi6fykU9Psmk&usp=drive_copy):
> Building condition trade-offs: Although this shift lowers Jackson's expected enrollment, Robert Gray offers better building conditions to welcome Maplewood families.
