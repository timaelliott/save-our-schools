## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Changes from K-8 to K-5; keeps both neighborhood area and Creative Science choice program; current 6th and 7th graders reassigned to Harrison Park MS or may return to their neighborhood MS.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/bridger_creative_science.pdf p.3]:
> Bridger Creative Science would change from a K-8 to a K-5 school. The school would keep both its neighborhood school area and its Creative Science special-focus choice program. Current middle grade (6th and 7th) students would be reassigned to Harrison Park Middle School or could choose to return to their neighborhood middle school.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/bridger_creative_science.pdf p.3]:
> Steady student numbers: Changing to K-5 boosts middle school enrollment in neighborhood schools while protecting its unique learning program.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/bridger_creative_science.pdf p.3]:
> Preserving student enrollment: Keeping both the neighborhood area and the special-focus choice program protects student enrollment and honors the school's unique community history. Southeast Guiding Coalition: Because of many recent changes, the decision was made to preserve the special-focus choice portion of Bridger Creative Science to ensure steady support after many other changes.
