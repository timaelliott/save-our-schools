## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Changes from K-8 to neighborhood K-5; 6-8 moves to Harriet Tubman MS; full seismic retrofit completed Summer 2026; may receive Sabin SES students.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/vernon.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1o4hfJxeWmEb2bMlWQpb2EKFDdeBmnfOT&usp=drive_copy):
> Vernon would change from a K-8 school to a neighborhood K-5 school. Middle grade (6th-8th) students would move to Harriet Tubman Middle School.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/vernon.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1o4hfJxeWmEb2bMlWQpb2EKFDdeBmnfOT&usp=drive_copy):
> Comprehensive middle school access: Changing Vernon from a K-8 to a K-5 model provides middle grade students access to Harriet Tubman Middle School, helping keep student numbers steady at Tubman. Seismically upgraded building: Vernon received a full seismic retrofit in Summer 2026, offering strong building conditions for elementary learning.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/vernon.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1o4hfJxeWmEb2bMlWQpb2EKFDdeBmnfOT&usp=drive_copy):
> Middle grade enrollment support: Moving middle grades to Harriet Tubman supports middle grades enrollment across the cluster.

**Board-member alternatives and advocacy (Oct 20, 2026):**
