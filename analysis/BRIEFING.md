## (a) Bottom line

1. **What is proposed.** Scenario A closes 14 schools and Scenario B closes 11. The same 11 close in both; A adds Lewis, Rose City Park and Stephenson [BB:14505788 p.2](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14505788?org=915#page=2). Both scenarios also include:
   - seven K-8→K-5 conversions;
   - ending MLC's 9–12 program;
   - moving Odyssey;
   - consolidating dual-language immersion (DLI);
   - 38 (A) or 34 (B) boundary changes [BB:14505788 p.1–2](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14505788?org=915#page=1).

   About 27% (A) or 24% (B) of K-8 students would change schools [BB:14511742 p.44](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14511742?org=915#page=44).
2. **Decision path.** The Superintendent recommends on Nov 17 and the Board votes Dec 15 [BB:14511742 p.45](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14511742?org=915#page=45). Armstrong told the Board it can accept the recommendation, "accept it with some changes or choose to defer this work to a later time" [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@3:11:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zOUH2Jq7Owo&t=11490s). Most changes start fall 2027; Sellwood and Marysville close in 2028 [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/sellwood.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1DDXzwTxlN46KRYVZvsNkzNDIIGHfYUcO&usp=drive_copy) [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/marysville.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1ZJXNeZJHjc7sKHR0E_iiA7XJEGXf0NsQ&usp=drive_copy).
3. **PPS's core case is program sufficiency, not savings.** Enrollment is 6,086 below 2018-19 and is projected to fall another 3,446 by 2031-32 [BB:14357211 p.9](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14357211?org=915#page=9). With no action, only 26% of schools and 48% of students would be at or above the size thresholds in 2031-32. Scenario A raises this to 67%/80% and B to 57%/73% [BB:14505788 p.1](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14505788?org=915#page=1).
4. **No savings estimate has been published.** A director asked what closing a school saves; staff answered only on layoffs: "The number of potential layoffs can not be determined at this time" [BB:14493920 p.7](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14493920?org=915#page=7). Armstrong has said "closing schools is not a cost-saving strategy" [YT:h_0YMlJnXPk@1:26:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h_0YMlJnXPk&t=5164s). A fiscal impact statement is promised with the Nov 17 recommendation [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@3:52:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zOUH2Jq7Owo&t=13948s). **Analysis:** plausible annual savings look like roughly 8–17% of the projected $65.2M 2027-28 gap (see e1).
5. **The thresholds are acknowledged stopgaps.** Thresholds are 360 (K-5), 500 (K-8/MS) and 1,100 (HS) [BB:14511742 p.10](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14511742?org=915#page=10). Franco called them "a bridge … not what we would say is ideal state" [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@1:38:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zOUH2Jq7Owo&t=5883s). The Board-commissioned equity audit recommended higher floors and warned they "should not be used as automatic closure thresholds" [BB:3997427 p.145](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/3997427?org=915#page=145).
6. **Several schools the plan creates or relies on are projected below threshold.** In 2031-32 these include converted K-5s Sunnyside (235), Vernon (323), Bridger (305) and Laurelhurst (359), and receiving middle schools Tubman (455) and Beaumont (498) [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/sunnyside.pdf p.4](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1A9NQhIiZOvxn2EYFwrVIj_eVJqfNQgKx&usp=drive_copy) [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/vernon.pdf p.4](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1o4hfJxeWmEb2bMlWQpb2EKFDdeBmnfOT&usp=drive_copy) [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/bridger_creative_science.pdf p.4](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1afLaZsAX9FYkVD12ZHXPx_RiOkjOYjfE&usp=drive_copy) [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/laurelhurst.pdf p.4](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1c-16JnzIlJyYpZrjHQEGE-KbvcyDj96B&usp=drive_copy) [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/harriet_tubman.pdf p.4](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1xmwGeM5Mp_JxLX-X4mfW_y19bTsVZx6R&usp=drive_copy) [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/beaumont.pdf p.4](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1gZJ-9jIGGmybagFBWmFTn1h-FKcbIFm_&usp=drive_copy).
7. **Equity analysis is missing.** Res. 7307 requires a "standardized equity impact analysis" for each scenario [BB:3997273 p.2](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/3997273?org=915#page=2). None has been released; only aggregate percentages are available [BB:14505788 p.1](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14505788?org=915#page=1). Six Title I schools close in both scenarios [BB:3997157 p.1](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/3997157?org=915#page=1) [NEWS:NEWS-20261006-katu-2](https://katu.com/news/project-education/pps-plans-would-close-six-schools-serving-more-low-income-families-title-one-inequity-poor-portland-public-school-teachers-students-parents-money-budget-funding).
8. **Bond and seismic money meets the closure list.**
   - Rose City Park has a planned full-campus seismic retrofit and closes in A, for "Severe building conditions" [BB:3990267 p.9](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/3990267?org=915#page=9) [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/rose_city_park.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1PuP0VaaG1rh4xC7Sjx4fkwHYGH_ZFJzH&usp=drive_copy).
   - Beach has a planned retrofit and closes in both scenarios [BB:3990267 p.13](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/3990267?org=915#page=13).
   - Marysville's grant-funded retrofit has just finished, and it closes too [BB:3996450 p.1–2](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/3996450?org=915#page=1) [NEWS:NEWS-20261009-kgw-1](https://www.kgw.com/article/news/education/18-million-spent-upgrading-portland-schools-that-could-now-close/283-72bc82cb-b20b-4c76-935a-a31c6af11f03).
9. **Forecast risk is real.** The Southeast Guiding Coalition (SEGC) boundary process is PPS's last comparable exercise. Its projections "overestimated enrollment at 12 of 20 schools" [BB:3997427 p.6](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/3997427?org=915#page=6). Fall 2026 enrollment is running about 700 below last year's count [BB:14493920 p.32](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14493920?org=915#page=32).
10. **The Board is split about 4–3 toward acting.**
    - Wang, DePass, Chase-Miller and Sullivan support acting at scale.
    - Splitt wants a smaller package and no additions. On Oct 20 she proposed her own: close or merge the 6 smallest schools, phased [YT:xByUlijpcnE@5:02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=18145s).
    - La Forte is undecided pending cost data. On Oct 20 she said "I'm not sold" and asked for "breathing room" on the December date [YT:xByUlijpcnE@6:28:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=23313s) [YT:xByUlijpcnE@6:29:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=23370s).
    - Engelsman calls the plan "mayhem" [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@2:56:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zOUH2Jq7Owo&t=10603s) (`people/README.md`). On Oct 20 she said the scenarios don't "line up with what we require" [YT:xByUlijpcnE@5:40:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=20436s).
    - Oct 20 savings figures (Armstrong): A about $14M (about 97 FTE preserved), B about $11M (76 FTE), against about 223 FTE of school cuts with no closures [YT:xByUlijpcnE@1:14:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=4473s) [YT:xByUlijpcnE@1:14:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=4499s) (`meetings/2026-10-20_xByUlijpcnE.md`).

    **Analysis:** some package probably passes; its size and amendments are open.
11. **Leverage points.**
    - The Oct 9–29 sessions and the scenario survey [BB:14511742 p.46](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14511742?org=915#page=46) [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/scenarios.html](https://www.pps.net/about/portland-public-schools-information/rightsize/scenarios).
    - The Nov 4 feedback summary [BB:14511742 p.45](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14511742?org=915#page=45).
    - The Nov 17 fiscal statement.
    - The Board's own closure policy (6.10.030-P), which, as a director described it, requires a closure report with "projected overall cost or savings … including impact on other schools and potential transportation costs" [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:55:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AAWpafO4vvQ&t=6936s) *(Engelsman, medium)*.

## (d) PPS's stated rationale and the evidence behind it

**Enrollment [Doc].**
- PRC counted 42,622 K-12 students in fall 2025, "just over 6,000" below the pre-pandemic baseline [BB:14493920 p.15](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14493920?org=915#page=15).
- PPS projects −12% from 2025-26 to 2035-36 [BB:3988980 p.6](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/3988980?org=915#page=6).
- PPS also projects a 3,446 decrease by 2031-32 [BB:14357211 p.9](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14357211?org=915#page=9).
- The decline is concentrated in the elementary grades and "will reach the middle grades in the next five years" [BB:3997426 p.2](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/3997426?org=915#page=2).
- Capture rose from 74.4% (2019) to 76.6% (2025), against 82.2% in 2015 [BB:14493920 p.15](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14493920?org=915#page=15).

**Status quo outcome [Doc].**
- In 2031-32 with no action, 8 of 45 K-5s would have 360+ students [BB:14357211 p.11](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14357211?org=915#page=11).
- 7 of 15 middle schools would be under 400 [BB:3997425 p.17](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/3997425?org=915#page=17).
- The KPI for Title I middle schools above threshold falls from 29% to 0% [BB:14430171 p.14](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14430171?org=915#page=14).

**Budget [Doc].**
