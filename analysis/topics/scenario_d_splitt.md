# Splitt's "Scenario D" (draft framework) compared with the Nov 13 recommendation

**Source.** Director Christy Splitt's draft is archived at `sources/board_members/splitt/`. The version archived is the one fetched Nov 17, 2026.

The draft calls itself "a draft framework developed by one school board member". It is "a living document". It was first posted Oct 23, 2026, and "PPS staff have not yet reviewed this document" [BRD:splitt/2026-11-17_splitt_scenario_d_draft.pdf p.1].

All figures below are Splitt's own estimates unless they are cited to PPS. A row-by-row table is in `analysis/data/scenario_d_splitt_2026-11-17.csv`.

## What it proposes

**Overall approach.** It merges the smallest schools into neighbors. Splitt counts "16 PPS buildings projected to have 250 or fewer students in the fall of 2027" and says Scenario D reduces that to four. It also prioritizes closing buildings with costly seismic needs, including two unreinforced masonry schools [BRD:splitt/2026-11-17_splitt_scenario_d_draft.pdf p.1].

**Scale** [BRD:splitt/2026-11-17_splitt_scenario_d_draft.pdf p.1]:
- about 1,231 students move from a closed school;
- about 1,670 more change schools or attend a different school than planned;
- that is about 10% of students, against 27% under Scenario A and 24% under B;
- 37 of 81 schools see some change.

**The mergers.** The section is headed "Six Mergers". Five are school mergers, and the sixth building closure is East Sylvan, when Odyssey moves to MLC.

**Not merged in Scenario D:**
- **Sabin.** It was dropped from an earlier version: "Sabin has an Social Emotional Skills program, which requires at least 4 classrooms, and fitting that program into either King or Vernon exceeds available space" [BRD:splitt/2026-11-17_splitt_scenario_d_draft.pdf p.7]. The recommendation sends all of Sabin to King, at 572–590 students against King's 584 [BB:14695445 p.34](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695445?org=915#page=34).
- **Beach.** It stays open. Splitt adds that if Rosa Parks cannot hold the combined student body, a merger across Peninsula, Chief Joseph and Beach should be discussed [BRD:splitt/2026-11-17_splitt_scenario_d_draft.pdf p.3].
- **Marysville, Rose City Park, Lewis, Maplewood, Stephenson, James John and Sellwood MS.**

**Other changes** [BRD:splitt/2026-11-17_splitt_scenario_d_draft.pdf p.4] [BRD:splitt/2026-11-17_splitt_scenario_d_draft.pdf p.5]:
- **K-8s converting to K-5** (same as the recommendation): Beverly Cleary, Laurelhurst, Sunnyside, Faubion, Vernon and Bridger. Splitt gives no separate timing for Bridger; the recommendation waits until fall 2028.
- **Sunnyside** students may all move on to Hosford together.
- **Beaumont's Spanish immersion** moves to Roseway Heights in phases. Current 6th–7th graders stay at Beaumont through 2028-29, and Irvington's 5th graders go to Tubman.
- **César Chávez** neighborhood middle schoolers go to George.
- **Spanish immersion** otherwise stays in place.
- **Skyline** graduates choose Roosevelt or Lincoln.
- **Maplewood graduates move to Robert Gray MS** instead of Jackson, about 54 students. Current students can continue at Jackson, and siblings can choose Jackson. The recommendation makes no change to Maplewood's feeder.

**Policy package** [BRD:splitt/2026-11-17_splitt_scenario_d_draft.pdf p.5] [BRD:splitt/2026-11-17_splitt_scenario_d_draft.pdf p.6]:
- Closed buildings may not become charter or private K-12 schools.
- Focus-option lotteries start in 1st or 2nd grade, with caps per sending school and weighting for low-income students and students with disabilities.
- A DLI work group in 2027-28.
- A **Southwest boundary work group in 2027-28.** It covers Ainsworth (projected 550 in Sept 2027), Bridlemile (419), Rieke (251), Hayhurst (274), **Maplewood (278)**, Capitol Hill (310), Markham (312) and Stephenson (271). It may consider middle and high school enrollment "as well possible mergers".
- A three-year enrollment pilot funded by four Wells-cluster school communities, with no PPS action needed.
- An annual enrollment report flagging schools under 360 (elementary), 500 (middle) or 1,100 (high).
- Bond priority for receiving schools that take in more than 100 students.
- No vote without class-size lists, per-change equity and fiscal analysis, and transition plans.

## Analysis

**Overlap.** The recommendation's fall-2027 closures match Scenario D in Peninsula, Irvington and Buckman.
- The recommendation adds Beach, Sabin and Creston-to-Atkinson in 2027.
- It adds Woodmere, Lewis, Marysville and Rose City Park in 2028.
- Scenario D merges Creston and Woodmere in a different way and leaves Sabin and Beach open.

**Overcrowding.** Scenario D avoids most of the recommendation's pinch points: Chief Joseph (Beach), King (Sabin), Atkinson (Creston) and Whitman (Woodmere, which it splits with Lewis). It keeps the same pressure points at Sunnyside and Rosa Parks.

The Rosa Parks numbers conflict. Splitt's 609-seat capacity differs from PPS's 499 [BB:14695445 p.82](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695445?org=915#page=82); at 461 students, that is 76% versus 92% full. Splitt herself flags that Rosa Parks may not fit [BRD:splitt/2026-11-17_splitt_scenario_d_draft.pdf p.3].

**Maplewood.**
- Scenario D keeps Maplewood open, but it is the only plan of the three that changes Maplewood's middle school, sending graduates to Gray instead of Jackson.
- It puts Maplewood into a Southwest boundary review that could consider "possible mergers".

The recommendation leaves Maplewood unchanged but lists it among four schools to "monitor closely" [BB:14695483 p.43](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14695483?org=915#page=43).

**Status.** Splitt has said she will likely vote no on Scenarios A and B [NEWS:NEWS-20261020-oregonian-1](https://www.oregonlive.com/education/2026/10/2-portland-public-schools-board-members-pitch-alternatives-to-closing-14-schools.html). Scenario D is her basis for a compromise or an amendment, not a staff-modeled option. Its numbers have not been reviewed by PPS. Splitt credits rightsizepdx.com's comparison tool for an audit of it [BRD:splitt/2026-11-17_splitt_scenario_d_draft.pdf p.7].
