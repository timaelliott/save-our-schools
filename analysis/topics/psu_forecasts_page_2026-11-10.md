## How it relates to what the archive already had

- The cell values in both tabs match the sheet the repo owner supplied on Nov 10 [PPS:psu_prc/forecast_history_sheet/six_years_of_psu_projections_2026-11-10.xlsx](https://github.com/timaelliott/save-our-schools/blob/main/sources/psu_prc/forecast_history_sheet/six_years_of_psu_projections_2026-11-10.xlsx). The 12,935 forecast rows and 311 enrollment rows are identical.
- That resolves the open provenance question in `analysis/topics/psu_six_year_forecast_sheet.md`. The sheet is a PPS publication, posted on the rightsizing site. It is the forecast-history tool staff announced on Nov 4 [YT:9kX3vnfRlvY@3:48:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9kX3vnfRlvY&t=13731s).
- So the findings built on that sheet now rest on a PPS-published source. Among them: the 2026-27 edition cut Maplewood's 2031-32 forecast by 15% while raising Rieke's by 19% (`analysis/topics/psu_six_year_forecast_sheet.md`).
