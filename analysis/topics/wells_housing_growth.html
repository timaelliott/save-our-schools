How these numbers were made

Permits. City of Portland “Residential Building Permits” open data: every permit that creates new housing units, counted in units by calendar year issued, through October 1, 2026.

Home types. Houses are detached houses, townhouses, ADUs, duplexes and the few units added to existing small buildings. Apartments are new buildings of 5 or more units (apartments and condos).

Wells area is the Ida B. Wells High School attendance area from the Multnomah County / City of Portland school attendance map (where it is still labeled Wilson HS). PPS may draw its boundary slightly differently.

Schools. The six Wells cluster elementary schools. Distances are straight-line from each school’s address. Only City of Portland permits are counted, so Hayhurst and Maplewood, near the Washington County line, are somewhat undercounted.

2027–2030 forecast. The average yearly pace of 2024–2026 (2026 scaled to a full year) for each home type, plus Raleigh Crest’s 263 approved houses and townhouses spread evenly over 2027–2030 (the developer’s target finish is 2030). Pending permits are not added on top, because they are part of the normal pace.

Students. 0.45 PPS students per new single-family home, from PSU Population Research Center’s 2026–27 PPS enrollment forecast (Table 2.6).
