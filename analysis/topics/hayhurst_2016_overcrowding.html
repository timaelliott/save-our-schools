Portland Public Schools · Hayhurst Elementary, SW Portland

When Hayhurst Was Full

In 2016 PPS called Hayhurst overcrowded at 499 students and moved an entire program out of the building. Ten years later, PPS rates a projected 469-student Hayhurst “Ready / Minor Adjustments.”

How to read the comparison

PPS’s 2016 tables put Hayhurst’s room at roughly 490 to 520 students: 499 students was 101.8% of capacity, and 531 was 102.4%. The 2026 profile lists a functional capacity of 525. By either measure, the projected 469 would be close to the 2015 level, and far above Hayhurst’s highest K-5 enrollment since at least 2010 (396, in fall 2019).

The 2015 count included Odyssey’s 6th–8th graders, and PPS has changed how it counts capacity since then. Even so, the district that called 499 students in this building overcrowded now lists one urgent fix, $30,100 of kitchen equipment, for 469.

Fifteen general classrooms cannot hold three classes per grade (18) for six grades. At 469 students, about 78 per grade, three classes per grade is what the enrollment would need.
