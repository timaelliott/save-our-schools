Maplewood beat both estimates

Of PPS’s 45 K-5 schools, Maplewood had the second-largest enrollment gain from last fall (+17) and beat PSU’s forecast by 16 students, one of the larger misses in the district. PPS still proposes to close it in both scenarios.

+17
Maplewood, Oct 2025 → Oct 1, 2026 (287 → 304). Only 10 of 45 K-5 schools grew.
+16
Maplewood above PSU’s 2026-27 forecast (288 forecast, 304 actual).
304
Maplewood today, more than Hayhurst (286) or Rieke (254), the two schools meant to absorb it.

Sources. Oct 1, 2026 actuals: PPS, “2026-2027 October 1st Enrollment” by grade and program. Oct 2025 counts and the 2026-27 forecast: PSU Population Research Center, Enrollment Forecasts 2026-27 to 2035-36, Table 5.5 (historic 2025-26 and forecast 2026-27 columns). Closure status: PPS Scenario A/B family guides, Oct 2026.

K-5 elementary schools only (K-8s, middle and high schools excluded). Schools with language-immersion programs are shown as one total. “Southwest” is the Wells-cluster elementaries plus the Lincoln-cluster schools west of the river (PPS’s West region). Data: analysis/data/psu_2026-27_forecast_vs_oct1_2026_actual.csv.
