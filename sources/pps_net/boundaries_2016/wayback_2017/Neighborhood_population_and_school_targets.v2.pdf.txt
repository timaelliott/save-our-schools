*Assumptions: A school boundary should result in a sufficient neighborhood population to meet school size
targets. For modeling purposes, it is assumed that 25% of students will attend other PPS schools, including
charters, alternatives, focus options or the neighborhood schools where students were residents before
moving to the current neighborhood. A further assumption is that 10% of school enrollment to be made up of
non-neighborhood students, including students attending special education programs, approved for hardship
transfer or former resident students. Some schools (highlighted in orange) house choice programs open to
students from other neighborhoods.
Enrollment counts do not include PK students, including those attending Head Start programs
Enrollment counts for Grant, Madison and Lincoln high schools include Jefferson dual assignment areas
