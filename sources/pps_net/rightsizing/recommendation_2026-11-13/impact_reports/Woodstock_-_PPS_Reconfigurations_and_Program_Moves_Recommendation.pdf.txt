     WHAT’S HAPPENING?

      ●​   Woodstock would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school and receive the Mandarin
           Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program from Clark K-5 beginning in 2028-29.




WHY IS PPS CONSIDERING THIS?

●​   Strengthening DLI learning: Moving the Mandarin DLI program from Clark to Woodstock
     strengthens the DLI learning at a single school.
●​   Low program enrollment: Enrollment in Clark's Mandarin DLI program dropped below eight
     kindergarteners, making it difficult to keep as a stand-alone program.



WHAT ELSE WAS CONSIDERED?

●​   Keeping the program going: Concentrating Mandarin DLI programs at Woodstock expands
     program support and supports teacher collaboration.



WHAT WOULD STUDENTS GAIN?

●​   Vibrant language community: Bringing together the program at Woodstock builds one language
     program with enough students to continue and create a school-wide culture centered on language
     learning.
●​   Teacher teamwork: Enables DLI teachers to work together, plan together, and grow alongside
     fellow language educators.

ENROLLMENT AND TRANSFER

●​   PPS transfer students: K-4th grade students attending Clark Mandarin Immersion will enroll at
     Woodstock Mandarin Immersion or may exit DLI and transfer to their neighborhood school by
     home address.

ACADEMIC PROGRAM TRANSITIONS

●​   Special Education: Currently, Woodstock has one Learning Center class and one CB class.​
     With rightsizing, Woodstock's Special Education programming will go unchanged.
●​   Student and Family Supports: Student Services is reviewing school-based mental health support
     arrangements at Woodstock as district school-change plans are developed. This includes
     considering existing referral pathways and any additional student support needs.


 School transition supports: Teams from Central Office have developed a framework for implementing Rightsizing. This
 includes a menu of transition supports for all impacted school communities. This work is informed by lessons learned by
 districts across the country that have had successful school consolidations. The implementation framework includes
 guidance on merging academic programs and school-based teams, (such as Instructional Leadership, Climate, PTAs and
 others) tools, resources, and timelines. The framework includes timelines for merging school communities and
 onboarding staff, families and students to their new campuses. It also includes staffing timelines and plans for packing
 and moving furniture and instructional resources. This comprehensive framework will be shared with the community in
 December. If the Board of Education votes to proceed with Rightsizing for fall 2027, Central Office teams will begin
 working with school communities in January to implement transition supports appropriate to each community.
