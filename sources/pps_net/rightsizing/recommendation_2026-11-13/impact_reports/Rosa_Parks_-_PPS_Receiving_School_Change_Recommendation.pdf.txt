    WHAT’S HAPPENING?

     ●   Rosa Parks would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school.
     ●   Rosa Parks would expand its boundary to receive 100% of the closing Peninsula K-5
         boundary.
     ●   Rosa Parks would receive César Chávez K-5 students who opt-out of attending César
         Chávez Spanish Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program.




WHY IS PPS CONSIDERING THIS?

●   Updated school setup: Rosa Parks is an updated school with more room for students, an early
    learning classroom, SUN school, and community support programs, and an on-site food pantry.
●   Filling open classroom space: Receiving students from Peninsula (site closure) and César Chávez
    (not attending Spanish DLI) increases building use and helps keep steady student numbers at
    elementary enrollment in the Roosevelt cluster.



WHAT WOULD STUDENTS GAIN?

●   Upgraded learning spaces: Incoming students gain access to modern classrooms, updated
    building, and strong community support services.

ACADEMIC PROGRAM TRANSITIONS

●   Academic Programs: Based on current enrollment projections, there will be three to four
    classrooms at each grade-level, with blends not required. The Academics team will work with
    educators to plan curriculum for students who are in blends during the 2026-27 school year and
    may not be during the 2027-28 school year. All classrooms will be neighborhood English Scholar
    classrooms. Students will receive PE, Arts, and Library/Media instruction each week. Based on
    enrollment, the school will be supported by a full-time counselor. PPS Pre-K currently housed at
    Rosa Parks will remain intact.
●   Special Education: Currently, Rosa Parks is an Inclusive School Community with 2.5 Learning
    Center teachers. With rightsizing, Rosa Parks will continue to provide special education services
    through the Inclusive School model that is already being implemented at all three of the merging
    school sites. Rosa Parks will house six to seven Special Education spaces.
●   Student and Family Supports: Cemented Rose Therapeutics is identified as Rosa Parks'
    school-based mental health partner. Student Services is reviewing capacity and continuity of
    support for students who may transition from César Chávez. As district and county budgets allow,
    the SEI SUN program and other culturally-specific partnerships will continue to support the school
    community. Our childcare team is exploring an expansion of childcare capacity at Rosa Parks.

REINVESTMENTS FOR RECEIVING SCHOOLS
Schools that receive additional students through consolidation will be allocated a proportion of the total programmatic
savings that result from Rightsizing actions. This allocation will be distributed as discretionary FTE that can be used for
instructional coaching, academic intervention, school climate support, social work, or duty support. School principals, with
consultation from their school communities and approval from their Senior Directors of Instructional Leadership, will
determine how to use these additional investments.


  School transition supports: Teams from Central Office have developed a framework for implementing Rightsizing. This
  includes a menu of transition supports for all impacted school communities. This work is informed by lessons learned by
  districts across the country that have had successful school consolidations. The implementation framework includes
  guidance on merging academic programs and school-based teams, (such as Instructional Leadership, Climate, PTAs and
  others) tools, resources, and timelines. The framework includes timelines for merging school communities and
  onboarding staff, families and students to their new campuses. It also includes staffing timelines and plans for packing
  and moving furniture and instructional resources. This comprehensive framework will be shared with the community in
  December. If the Board of Education votes to proceed with Rightsizing for fall 2027, Central Office teams will begin
  working with school communities in January to implement transition supports appropriate to each community.
