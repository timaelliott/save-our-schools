     WHAT’S HAPPENING?

      ●​   Lent remains a focused K-5 Spanish Dual Language Immersion (DLI) school serving the
           Cleveland/Franklin area, and will welcome Spanish DLI students from Atkinson beginning
           in 2027-28.
      ●​   Lent neighborhood students who opt-out of Spanish DLI would enroll at Kelly E.S.
           beginning in 2028-29 and moving on to Brentwood M.S. instead of Kellogg.
      ●​   Lent neighborhood students would continue to have direct access to Spanish DLI.




WHY IS PPS CONSIDERING THIS?

●​   Language programs serving each area: Concentrates Spanish DLI into schools serving each area
     across the district to strengthen language immersion.
●​   Reaching sustainable enrollment: Bringing Atkinson and Lent Spanish DLI communities together
     reaches sustainable target enrollment levels (400+ students).



WHAT ELSE WAS CONSIDERED?

●​   Room for more students: Lent’s spacious building is ideally suited for a DLI program serving the
     area.



WHAT WOULD STUDENTS GAIN?

●​   Unifying Programs: Unifies DLI programs into a single school where language teachers plan and
     work together.
●​   Supporting the school over time: Secures sustainable enrollment and stable programs.

ENROLLMENT AND TRANSFER

●​   Neighborhood students: Incoming K students residing in the Lent attendance area who do not
     attend Lent Spanish Immersion will enroll at Kelly E.S. 5th grade students who live in the Lent
     attendance area will enroll at Brentwood M.S. for 6th grade.
●​   PPS transfer students: K-4th grade students attending Atkinson E.S. Spanish Immersion will enroll
     at Lent E.S. Spanish Immersion. Incoming K students residing in the Lent attendance area with a
     sibling who had attended Marysville E.S have a guaranteed transfer to join their sibling at Arleta
     E.S. Native Spanish speakers residing in the Cleveland/Franklin attendance areas may directly
     enroll at Lent Spanish Immersion. Incoming K students residing in the historical Lent attendance
     area may directly enroll at Lent Spanish Immersion. Incoming 1st-4th grade students residing in the
     historical Lent attendance area may directly enroll at Lent Spanish Immersion if they meet language
     requirements. All other students must apply to Lent Spanish Immersion through the lottery, with
     preference given to students residing in the Cleveland/Franklin attendance areas.

ACADEMIC PROGRAM TRANSITIONS

●​   Academic Programs: Lent will maintain and expand its whole school Spanish Dual Language
     Immersion model. DLI instruction will continue to follow the 90-10 model. Based on current
     enrollment projections, there will be two to three classrooms at each grade-level, with blends not
     required. Students will receive PE, Arts, and Library/Media instruction each week. The majority of
     staff in the building will be Spanish-bilingual. Based on enrollment, the school will be supported by
     a full-time counselor. PPS Pre-K programming already located at Lent will remain intact.
●​   Special Education: Currently, Lent has one Learning Center class. With rightsizing, there will be no
     change to Lent's current special education service delivery model.
●​   Student and Family Supports: Morrison Child and Family Services is identified as Lent's
     school-based mental health partner for 2026–27. Student Services is working with mental health
     partners to review capacity and continuity of services for students transitioning with the Spanish
     Immersion program from Atkinson. As district and county budgets allow, the IRCO SUN program

REINVESTMENTS FOR RECEIVING SCHOOLS
Schools that receive additional students through consolidation will be allocated a proportion of the total programmatic
savings that result from Rightsizing actions. This allocation will be distributed as discretionary FTE that can be used for
instructional coaching, academic intervention, school climate support, social work, or duty support. School principals, with
consultation from their school communities and approval from their Senior Directors of Instructional Leadership, will
determine how to use these additional investments.


  School transition supports: Teams from Central Office have developed a framework for implementing Rightsizing. This
  includes a menu of transition supports for all impacted school communities. This work is informed by lessons learned by
  districts across the country that have had successful school consolidations. The implementation framework includes
  guidance on merging academic programs and school-based teams, (such as Instructional Leadership, Climate, PTAs and
  others) tools, resources, and timelines. The framework includes timelines for merging school communities and
  onboarding staff, families and students to their new campuses. It also includes staffing timelines and plans for packing
  and moving furniture and instructional resources. This comprehensive framework will be shared with the community in
  December. If the Board of Education votes to proceed with Rightsizing for fall 2027, Central Office teams will begin
  working with school communities in January to implement transition supports appropriate to each community.
