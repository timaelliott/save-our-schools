    WHAT’S HAPPENING?

     ●   Arleta would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school.
     ●   Arleta would welcome the entire neighborhood school area and student body from
         Marysville.




WHY IS PPS CONSIDERING THIS?

●   Arleta chosen for building size: While building conditions at Arleta and Marysville were similar,
    Marysville was too small to fit both school communities together.



WHAT ELSE WAS CONSIDERED?

●   Large school space: Arleta has 28 regular classrooms and can comfortably hold up to 653 students.
    This proposal keeps the enrollment well above current enrollment but well below the most
    students the building can hold.
●   Major building upgrades: Arleta is getting a brand-new heating and cooling (HVAC) system in
    Summer 2027 and has a full kitchen setup.



WHAT WOULD STUDENTS GAIN?

●   Upgraded learning spaces: Students would gain modern air quality and heating, and large spaces
    for learning and play.
●   Stronger school programs: Combining communities allows the school to offer programs that can
    continue over time and support student services.

ACADEMIC PROGRAM TRANSITIONS

●   Academic Programs: School year 2028-29: Based on current enrollment projections, there will be 3
    to 4 classrooms at each grade-level, with blends not required. The Academics team will work with
    educators to plan curriculum for students who are in blends during the 2027-28 school year and
    may not be during the 2028-29 school year. Students will receive PE, Arts, and Library/Media
    instruction each week. Based on enrollment, the school will be supported by a full-time counselor.
●   Special Education: Currently, Arleta has one Learning Center and one SES classroom. With
    rightsizing, Arleta will maintain one 3-5 SES classroom and receive the students from Marysville
    served in the Inclusive School Community model. This model will be supported at Arleta. In total
    Arleta will then have four special education teachers and one SES teacher. This will result in four to
    five classrooms.
●   Student and Family Supports: Currently Arleta offers before and after school programming.
    Our RESJ team is exploring an expansion of childcare capacity at Arleta. Currently, there is SUN
    programming at Arleta. Continued SUN programming will depend on County, PPS, and City
    budget process. Currently, there are two partners at Arleta. RESJ partnerships continuing or
    expanding will be dependent on the PPS budget process. Currently, Morrison Child and Family
    Services is identified as Arleta's School-Based Mental Health Partner for 2026–27.

REINVESTMENTS FOR RECEIVING SCHOOLS
Schools that receive additional students through consolidation will be allocated a proportion of the total programmatic
savings that result from Rightsizing actions. This allocation will be distributed as discretionary FTE that can be used for
instructional coaching, academic intervention, school climate support, social work, or duty support. School principals, with
consultation from their school communities and approval from their Senior Directors of Instructional Leadership, will
determine how to use these additional investments.


  School transition supports: Teams from Central Office have developed a framework for implementing Rightsizing. This
  includes a menu of transition supports for all impacted school communities. This work is informed by lessons learned by
  districts across the country that have had successful school consolidations. The implementation framework includes
  guidance on merging academic programs and school-based teams, (such as Instructional Leadership, Climate, PTAs and
  others) tools, resources, and timelines. The framework includes timelines for merging school communities and
  onboarding staff, families and students to their new campuses. It also includes staffing timelines and plans for packing
  and moving furniture and instructional resources. This comprehensive framework will be shared with the community in
  December. If the Board of Education votes to proceed with Rightsizing for fall 2027, Central Office teams will begin
  working with school communities in January to implement transition supports appropriate to each community.
