Rose City Park E.S.                                                               Changes beginning in
                                                                                       2028-29




    WHAT’S CHANGING?

     ●   Rose City Park Elementary would close as a school building.
     ●   100% of its neighborhood K-5 students would move to Scott K-5.
     ●   Special Education students receiving Learning Center support would continue to receive
         support at Scott K-5.
     ●   Vietnamese Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program would move to Vestal K-5.
     ●   Middle grade Vietnamese DLI students would remain at Roseway Heights Middle School.




WHY IS PPS CONSIDERING THIS?

●   Urgent building repairs: Rose City Park faces major building risks, including very old building
    systems and critical maintenance failures requiring a full seismic overhaul.
●   Avoiding temporary moves: Bringing together Rose City Park neighborhood students with Scott
    K-5 and Vietnamese DLI students with Vestal K-5 students, avoiding expensive temporary
    construction moves.



WHAT ELSE WAS CONSIDERED?

●   Building Condition: Severe building conditions was a major consideration for school closure.



WHAT WOULD STUDENTS GAIN?

●   Safer building conditions: Students move out of an aging building into safer, updated learning
    environments.
●   Protected language path: Vietnamese DLI continues at Vestal with no change to middle school
    plans.

ENROLLMENT AND TRANSFER

 ●   Neighborhood students: K-4th grade students and incoming K students residing in the Rose City
     Park attendance area will enroll at Scott E.S.
 ●   PPS transfer students: K-4th grade students attending Rose City Park but not residing in the
     attendance area will be enrolled at Scott E.S. or may transfer to their neighborhood school by
     home address. K-4th grade students attending Rose City Park Vietnamese Immersion will enroll at
     Vestal E.S. Vietnamese Immersion or may exit DLI and transfer to their neighborhood school by
     home address

School transition supports: Teams from Central Office have developed a framework for implementing Rightsizing. This
includes a menu of transition supports for all impacted school communities. This work is informed by lessons learned by
districts across the country that have had successful school consolidations. The implementation framework includes
guidance on merging academic programs and school-based teams, (such as Instructional Leadership, Climate, PTAs and
others) tools, resources, and timelines. The framework includes timelines for merging school communities and
onboarding staff, families and students to their new campuses. It also includes staffing timelines and plans for packing
and moving furniture and instructional resources. This comprehensive framework will be shared with the community in
December. If the Board of Education votes to proceed with Rightsizing for fall 2027, Central Office teams will begin
working with school communities in January to implement transition supports appropriate to each community.
