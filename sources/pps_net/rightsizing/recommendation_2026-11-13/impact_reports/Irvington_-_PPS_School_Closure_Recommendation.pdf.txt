PORTLAND PUBLIC SCHOOLS / SCHOOL CLOSURE RECOMMENDATION



Irvington E.S.                                                                    Changes beginning in
                                                                                       2027-28




    WHAT’S CHANGING?

     ●   Irvington School would close as a neighborhood K-5 school.
     ●   K-5 students would join with Beverly Cleary K-5 students at Irvington in 2027-28 and then
         move together to the Beverly Cleary building in 2028-29 after seismic upgrades are
         complete.
     ●   Special Education students receiving Learning Center support would continue to receive
         support at Beverly Cleary K-5.
     ●   Middle grade (6th-8th) students would move from Tubman to Beaumont Middle School.




WHY IS PPS CONSIDERING THIS?

●   Low enrollment numbers: Irvington is expected to drop to just 223 students by 2031-32
    (well below target levels).
●   Updated buildings: Bringing together Irvington into Beverly Cleary expands student access to
    updated buildings and eliminates split middle school paths.



WHAT ELSE WAS CONSIDERED?

●   Central location: Beverly Cleary offers a more central location than Irvington.
●   Temporary construction site: Irvington will serve as a temporary "swing site" for the combined
    student population during Beverly Cleary's Summer 2027 seismic upgrades.



WHAT WOULD STUDENTS GAIN?

●   Better learning spaces: Students move into an upgraded Beverly Cleary building.
●   Full student support: Bringing communities together creates a stronger student body size that
    supports more stable staffing.

ENROLLMENT AND TRANSFER

 ●   Neighborhood students: K-4th grade students and incoming K students residing in the Irvington
     attendance area will consolidate with Beverly Cleary K-5 students at Irvington in 2027-28, and
     move to the Beverly Cleary building in 2028-29. 5th grade students residing in the Irvington
     attendance area will enroll at Beaumont M.S. for 6th grade
 ●   PPS transfer students: K-4th grade students attending Irvington but not residing in the attendance
     area will be enrolled with Beverly Cleary students or may transfer to their neighborhood school by
     home address. 5th grade students residing in the Irvington attendance area with a sibling
     attending Harriet Tubman M.S. have a guaranteed transfer to Harriet Tubman for 6th grade.

School transition supports: Teams from Central Office have developed a framework for implementing Rightsizing. This
includes a menu of transition supports for all impacted school communities. This work is informed by lessons learned by
districts across the country that have had successful school consolidations. The implementation framework includes
guidance on merging academic programs and school-based teams, (such as Instructional Leadership, Climate, PTAs and
others) tools, resources, and timelines. The framework includes timelines for merging school communities and
onboarding staff, families and students to their new campuses. It also includes staffing timelines and plans for packing
and moving furniture and instructional resources. This comprehensive framework will be shared with the community in
December. If the Board of Education votes to proceed with Rightsizing for fall 2027, Central Office teams will begin
working with school communities in January to implement transition supports appropriate to each community.
