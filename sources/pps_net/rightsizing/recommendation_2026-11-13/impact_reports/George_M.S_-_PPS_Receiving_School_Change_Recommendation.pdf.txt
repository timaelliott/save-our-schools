    WHAT’S HAPPENING?

     ●   George Middle School would remain open as a neighborhood 6-8 middle school and
         become the regional 6-8 Spanish DLI school serving the Jefferson/Roosevelt area.
     ●   George would welcome Spanish DLI middle grade students from Ockley Green and César
         Chávez beginning in 2027-28.
     ●   George would welcome neighborhood middle grade students from Peninsula (shifting
         from Ockley Green) beginning with 6th graders in 2028-29.




WHY IS PPS CONSIDERING THIS?

●   Single language program serving the area: Brings together Spanish DLI into a single school serving
    the area leading directly to Roosevelt High School.
●   Recent building investments: George recently received bond-funded science lab upgrades,
    asbestos remediation (2025), and special education classroom upgrades.



WHAT ELSE WAS CONSIDERED?

●   Space limits: District teams considered all middle grade students in the region, but George did not
    have the room to also house middle grade students from Astor K-8.



WHAT WOULD STUDENTS GAIN?

●   Concentrated DLI pathway: Creates a more robust, sustainable language learning student group
    with shared DLI teaching teams.
●   Coherent route to middle and high schools: By making these shifts, it ensures that all students
    moving to Roosevelt High School are in a consistent route to middle and high school.
●   Updated learning spaces: Students benefit from updated science labs and upgraded classroom
    environments.

ACADEMIC PROGRAM TRANSITIONS

●   Academic Programs: George will maintain and expand its co-located English Scholar/Spanish Dual
    Language Immersion model. Based on current enrollment projections, there will be seven to nine
    sections at each grade level. George will continue to offer a range of electives including Band,
    String Orchestra, journalism, leadership, STEM, and Music Theater. Based on enrollment, the
    school will be supported by a full-time counselor.
●   Special Education: Currently, George has three Learning Center classes, two ISC classes, and one
    SES class.With rightsizing, students coming to George from other campuses will be served through
    the Special Education programming at George that is currently on site. George will have three
    Learning Center spaces, two ISC classrooms, and one SES classroom.
●   Student and Family Supports: Happy Valley Counseling and Multnomah County School-Based
    Mental Health are identified as George's School-Based Mental Health Partner. Student Services is
    working with partners to review capacity and continuity of services as additional middle-grade and
    Spanish immersion students join the school.

REINVESTMENTS FOR RECEIVING SCHOOLS
Schools that receive additional students through consolidation will be allocated a proportion of the total programmatic
savings that result from Rightsizing actions. This allocation will be distributed as discretionary FTE that can be used for
instructional coaching, academic intervention, school climate support, social work, or duty support. School principals, with
consultation from their school communities and approval from their Senior Directors of Instructional Leadership, will
determine how to use these additional investments.


  School transition supports: Teams from Central Office have developed a framework for implementing Rightsizing. This
  includes a menu of transition supports for all impacted school communities. This work is informed by lessons learned by
  districts across the country that have had successful school consolidations. The implementation framework includes
  guidance on merging academic programs and school-based teams, (such as Instructional Leadership, Climate, PTAs and
  others) tools, resources, and timelines. The framework includes timelines for merging school communities and
  onboarding staff, families and students to their new campuses. It also includes staffing timelines and plans for packing
  and moving furniture and instructional resources. This comprehensive framework will be shared with the community in
  December. If the Board of Education votes to proceed with Rightsizing for fall 2027, Central Office teams will begin
  working with school communities in January to implement transition supports appropriate to each community.
