    WHAT’S HAPPENING?

     ●   Faubion would change from a K-8 school to a neighborhood K-5 school.
     ●   Middle grade (6th-8th) students would move to Ockley Green Middle School.
     ●   The Bybee Lake Shelter school area would move from Faubion to Astor.




WHY IS PPS CONSIDERING THIS?

●   Richer middle school options: Providing a full suite of middle school electives, athletics, and
    algebra pathways has proven challenging within this K-8 model.
●   Focused early learning programming: Changing to a K-5 model allows Faubion to tailor its
    building and resources entirely to elementary development, including its robust PK program.



WHAT ELSE WAS CONSIDERED?

●   Avoiding overcrowding: District teams considered adding extra elementary attendance boundaries
    to Faubion, but it would have made Faubion too large or disrupted nearby communities at Vernon
    and Woodlawn.



WHAT WOULD STUDENTS GAIN?

●   Comprehensive middle school access: 6th-8th graders gain access to broader electives, sports
    teams, and middle school buildings at Ockley Green.
●   Elementary-focused school: Elementary students benefit from spaces and routines designed
    specifically for younger children.

ENROLLMENT AND TRANSFER

●   Neighborhood students: 5th-7th grade students residing in the Faubion attendance area will enroll
    at Ockley Green M.S.
●   PPS transfer students: 5th-7th grade students attending Faubion but not residing in the
    attendance area will enroll at Ockley Green E.S. or may transfer to their neighborhood school by
    home address.

ACADEMIC PROGRAM TRANSITIONS

●     Academic Programs: Faubion will house only grades K-5. Based on current enrollment projections,
      there will be two to three classrooms at each grade-level, with blends not required. Students will
      receive PE, Arts, and Library/Media instruction each week. Based on enrollment, the school will be
      supported by a full-time counselor. PPS Pre-K and Albina Early Head Start classrooms currently
      housed at Faubion will remain intact.
●     Special Education: Currently, Faubion has 2.5 Learning Center classrooms and one CB classroom.
      With rightsizing, there will be no changes to the Special Education service delivery model in the
      Faubion elementary program. Students in MS receiving Learning Center support will receive those
      services at Ockley Green. Faubion will continue to have one CB classroom, and two Learning
      Center classrooms.
●     Student and Family Supports: Currently, Trillium MH is Faubion's School-Based Mental Health
      Partner. Student Services is reviewing continuity of services for middle-grade students who may
      transition to Ockley Green.


    School transition supports: Teams from Central Office have developed a framework for implementing Rightsizing. This
    includes a menu of transition supports for all impacted school communities. This work is informed by lessons learned by
    districts across the country that have had successful school consolidations. The implementation framework includes
    guidance on merging academic programs and school-based teams, (such as Instructional Leadership, Climate, PTAs and
    others) tools, resources, and timelines. The framework includes timelines for merging school communities and
    onboarding staff, families and students to their new campuses. It also includes staffing timelines and plans for packing
    and moving furniture and instructional resources. This comprehensive framework will be shared with the community in
    December. If the Board of Education votes to proceed with Rightsizing for fall 2027, Central Office teams will begin
    working with school communities in January to implement transition supports appropriate to each community.
