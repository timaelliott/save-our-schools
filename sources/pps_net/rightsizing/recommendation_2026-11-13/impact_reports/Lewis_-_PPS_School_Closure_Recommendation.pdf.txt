Lewis E.S.                                                                      Changes beginning in
                                                                                     2028-29




    WHAT’S CHANGING?

     ●   Lewis School would close as a school building.
     ●   Neighborhood K-5 students would move to Duniway K-5.
     ●   Special Education students in the CB class and those receiving Learning Center support
         would continue to receive support at Duniway K-5.




WHY IS PPS CONSIDERING THIS?

●   Low enrollment numbers: Lewis enrollment is expected to drop to 201 students by 2031-32.
●   Better building conditions and space: Duniway offers a central location, more room for students,
    and better building conditions to serve both communities.



WHAT ELSE WAS CONSIDERED?

●   Duniway chosen as receiving school: After evaluating both school buildings, Duniway was chosen
    because it can support more students.



WHAT WOULD STUDENTS GAIN?

●   Updated building environment: Students move to an updated Duniway school with strong building
    systems.
●   Full student support: Bringing communities together creates a larger student body that supports
    programs and student supports.

ENROLLMENT AND TRANSFER

 ●   Neighborhood students: K-4th grade students and incoming K students residing in the Lewis
     attendance area will enroll at Duniway E.S.
 ●   PPS transfer students: K-4th grade students attending Lewis but not residing in the attendance
     area will be enrolled at Duniway E.S. or may transfer to their neighborhood school by home
     address.

School transition supports: Teams from Central Office have developed a framework for implementing Rightsizing. This
includes a menu of transition supports for all impacted school communities. This work is informed by lessons learned by
districts across the country that have had successful school consolidations. The implementation framework includes
guidance on merging academic programs and school-based teams, (such as Instructional Leadership, Climate, PTAs and
others) tools, resources, and timelines. The framework includes timelines for merging school communities and
onboarding staff, families and students to their new campuses. It also includes staffing timelines and plans for packing
and moving furniture and instructional resources. This comprehensive framework will be shared with the community in
December. If the Board of Education votes to proceed with Rightsizing for fall 2027, Central Office teams will begin
working with school communities in January to implement transition supports appropriate to each community.
