     WHAT’S HAPPENING?

      ●​   James John would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school.
      ●​   Its Spanish Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program would move to César Chávez K-5.




WHY IS PPS CONSIDERING THIS?

●​   Focused programming: Establishing César Chávez as a dedicated K–5 Dual Language Immersion
     (DLI) school creates a regional hub for Spanish language immersion while allowing James John to
     strengthen its focus on serving neighborhood students.



WHAT ELSE WAS CONSIDERED?

●​   Originally considered for closure, James John will remain open as neighborhood school enrollment
     may grow after the DLI program shifts.



WHAT WOULD STUDENTS GAIN?

●​   Concentrated DLI center: DLI students join a focused regional Spanish language center at César
     Chávez.

ACADEMIC PROGRAM TRANSITIONS

●​   Academic Programs: Based on current enrollment projections, there will be one to two classrooms
     at each grade level. Blends may be required in some cases. All classrooms will be neighborhood
     English Scholar classrooms. Students will receive PE, Arts, and Library/Media instruction each
     week. Based on enrollment, the school will be supported by a part-time counselor.
●​   Special Education: Currently, James John is an Inclusive School Community and has 3.5 Learning
     Center classes. With rightsizing, James John will continue to serve students with disabilities
     through an Inclusive Schools model.
●​   Student and Family Supports: Multnomah County School-Based Mental Health is identified as
     James John's school-based mental health partner. Student Services is reviewing continuity of
     support for students who may transition with the Spanish immersion program to César Chávez.


 School transition supports: Teams from Central Office have developed a framework for implementing Rightsizing. This
 includes a menu of transition supports for all impacted school communities. This work is informed by lessons learned by
 districts across the country that have had successful school consolidations. The implementation framework includes
 guidance on merging academic programs and school-based teams, (such as Instructional Leadership, Climate, PTAs and
 others) tools, resources, and timelines. The framework includes timelines for merging school communities and
 onboarding staff, families and students to their new campuses. It also includes staffing timelines and plans for packing
 and moving furniture and instructional resources. This comprehensive framework will be shared with the community in
 December. If the Board of Education votes to proceed with Rightsizing for fall 2027, Central Office teams will begin
 working with school communities in January to implement transition supports appropriate to each community.
