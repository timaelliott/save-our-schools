    WHAT’S HAPPENING?

     ●   Ockley Green would remain open as a neighborhood middle school.
     ●   Ockley Green would welcome 6th-8th grade students from Faubion K-5.
     ●   The Spanish Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program would move to George Middle
         School beginning in 2027-28.
     ●   The former Peninsula school area would shift to George Middle School beginning with 6th
         grade students in 2028-29.




WHY IS PPS CONSIDERING THIS?

●   DLI program serving the area: Moving middle grade Spanish DLI to George Middle School
    establishes one clear language program serving the area in North/Northeast Portland.
●   One high school destination: Welcoming middle grade students from Faubion helps keep steady
    student numbers at Ockley Green and eliminates split feeders to high schools.



WHAT ELSE WAS CONSIDERED?

●   Middle and high school cohesion: Fixes historical split route to high schools in the Jefferson cluster.



WHAT WOULD STUDENTS GAIN?

●   Clear high school pathways: Resolves split route to high schools so all Ockley Green students
    move together to Jefferson high school.
●   Expanded electives: Sustainable student enrollment supports full music programs, electives, and
    middle school athletics.

ENROLLMENT AND TRANSFER

●   Neighborhood students: 5th-7th grade students who reside in the Faubion attendance area will
    enroll at Ockley Green M.S. 5th grade students who reside in the Peninsula attendance area will
    enroll at George M.S. for 6th grade beginning in 2028-29.
●   PPS transfer students: 5th-7th grade students who attend Faubion but do not reside in the
    attendance area will enroll at Ockley Green M.S. or may transfer to their neighborhood school by

ACADEMIC PROGRAM TRANSITIONS

●   Academic Programs: Based on current enrollment projections, there will be six to eight sections at
    each grade level. All classrooms will be neighborhood English Scholar classrooms. Ockley Green
    will continue to offer multi-year pathways in Art, Band, and STEM, as well as a host of other
    electives. Based on enrollment, the school will be supported by a full-time counselor.
●   Special Education: Currently, Ockley Green has two Learning Center classes. With rightsizing, there
    will continue to have Learning Center classes assigned to support the needs of students with IEPs.
●   Student and Family Supports: Cemented Rose Therapeutics is identified as Ockley Green's
    school-based mental health partner. Student Services is coordinating with mental health partners
    to review capacity and continuity of support as students transition from Faubion and the Spanish
    immersion program changes locations.

REINVESTMENTS FOR RECEIVING SCHOOLS
Schools that receive additional students through consolidation will be allocated a proportion of the total programmatic
savings that result from Rightsizing actions. This allocation will be distributed as discretionary FTE that can be used for


                                                     PPS.NET/RIGHTSIZE                                 Ockley Green M.S.
instructional coaching, academic intervention, school climate support, social work, or duty support. School principals, with
consultation from their school communities and approval from their Senior Directors of Instructional Leadership, will
determine how to use these additional investments.


  School transition supports: Teams from Central Office have developed a framework for implementing Rightsizing. This
  includes a menu of transition supports for all impacted school communities. This work is informed by lessons learned by
  districts across the country that have had successful school consolidations. The implementation framework includes
  guidance on merging academic programs and school-based teams, (such as Instructional Leadership, Climate, PTAs and
  others) tools, resources, and timelines. The framework includes timelines for merging school communities and
  onboarding staff, families and students to their new campuses. It also includes staffing timelines and plans for packing
  and moving furniture and instructional resources. This comprehensive framework will be shared with the community in
  December. If the Board of Education votes to proceed with Rightsizing for fall 2027, Central Office teams will begin
  working with school communities in January to implement transition supports appropriate to each community.
