     WHAT’S HAPPENING?

      ●​   Sitton would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school.
      ●​   Its Spanish Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program would move to César Chávez K-5.




WHY IS PPS CONSIDERING THIS?

●​   Focused programming: Moving to a single-focus K-5 DLI school creates a regional Spanish
     language hub and allows Sitton to focus on its neighborhood school program.



WHAT ELSE WAS CONSIDERED?

●​   Building accessibility: Sitton was prepared to consolidate with James John primarily because its
     single-story layout provides total physical accessibility; however, James John remains open as a
     neighborhood K-5 school.



WHAT WOULD STUDENTS GAIN?

●​   Concentrated DLI center: DLI students join a focused regional Spanish language center at César
     Chávez.

ACADEMIC PROGRAM TRANSITIONS

●​   Academic Programs: Based on current enrollment projections, there will be two classrooms at each
     grade-level, with blends not required. All classrooms will be neighborhood English Scholar
     classrooms. Students will receive PE, Arts, and Library/Media instruction each week. Based on
     enrollment, the school will be supported by a part-time counselor.
●​   Special Education: Currently, Sitton is an Inclusive School Community with four Learning Center
     classes. With rightsizing, Sitton will continue to be an Inclusive School Community with Learning
     Center classes.
●​   Student and Family Supports: LifeWorks NW is identified as Sitton's school-based mental health
     partner. Student Services is reviewing continuity of support for students who may transition with
     the Spanish immersion program to César Chávez. Depending on Multnomah County and district
     budgets, the existing SUN program and other culturally-specific partnerships will remain intact.


 School transition supports: Teams from Central Office have developed a framework for implementing Rightsizing. This
 includes a menu of transition supports for all impacted school communities. This work is informed by lessons learned by
 districts across the country that have had successful school consolidations. The implementation framework includes
 guidance on merging academic programs and school-based teams, (such as Instructional Leadership, Climate, PTAs and
 others) tools, resources, and timelines. The framework includes timelines for merging school communities and
 onboarding staff, families and students to their new campuses. It also includes staffing timelines and plans for packing
 and moving furniture and instructional resources. This comprehensive framework will be shared with the community in
 December. If the Board of Education votes to proceed with Rightsizing for fall 2027, Central Office teams will begin
 working with school communities in January to implement transition supports appropriate to each community.
