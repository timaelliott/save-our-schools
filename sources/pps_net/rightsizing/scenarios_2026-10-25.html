Rightsizing Scenarios

PPS is exploring options to create a more sustainable school system where all students attend schools big enough to provide robust programming and supports. On this page you can learn more about the scenarios, explore school-level information and maps, and share your feedback.

Explore Scenario A and Scenario B and the proposed changes to schools, programs, boundaries and grade configurations across the district.
