Financial Update
Michelle Morrison & Maya Vinson
   ●​ Staff outlined the collaborative working structure between RISE and the Finance department,
      including weekly working sessions to monitor project cash flows, investment maturities, and cash
      needs.
   ●​ Finance controls and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) ensure all bond proceeds comply
      strictly with legal bond language, tax regulations, and internal/external audits.
   ●​ Accounting project codes are established in eBuilder and PeopleSoft (transitioning to Workday
      ERP), aligning project tracking directly with the bond structure.
   ●​ External funding sources—such as Portland Clean Energy Fund (PCEF) dollars, Energy Trust of
      Oregon (ETO) funds, and state grants—are accounted for in distinct sub-funds side-by-side with
      bond funds.
   ●​ Staff perform monthly reconciliations between PeopleSoft (system of record) and eBuilder project
      management records.
   ●​ Program-wide, total funds across the 2012–2025 bond programs stand at $4.6 billion, with spend
      increasing by $100 million and encumbrances rising 57% since last quarter, in part due to ERP
      and early work amendments for the modernization projects.
   ●​ The 2012 Bond is nearing final closeout with $84k in remaining contingency; recent allocations
      funded Grant High School flooring/boiler work and Franklin/Roosevelt blinds and playground
      equipment.
   ●​ The 2017 Bond is approximately 95% spent/encumbered. Chief Legal Officer Sharon Toncray
      provided an update on ongoing mediation with prime contractors regarding Benson modernization
      claims, noting that variance risks are covered within program reserves.
   ●​ The 2020 Bond spend increased by $23 million, with $17.6 million remaining in unallocated funds
      and $80 million in project contingency.
   ●​ The 2025 Bond spend/encumbrance doubled to 13% with $100 million in new encumbrances
      primarily driven by technology device refresh, Workday ERP contracting, and early modernization
      work.


Modernization Updates
TJ Hopkins & Sarah Norman
   ●​ Staff presented project updates using a new master schedule format consistent with monthly
      Board of Education reporting.
   ●​ Master schedule tracking indicates all major high school modernizations (Jefferson, Cleveland,
      and Ida B. Wells) remain on schedule for August 2029 student occupancy. All modernizations are
      tracking on-time delivery.
   ●​ On-site student swing strategies at Jefferson and Ida B. Wells will require post-occupancy
      demolition of old facilities and final athletic field construction through 2030.
   ●​ Permitting across projects is progressing on schedule through close coordination with the City of
      Portland Major Projects Group (MPG).
   ●​ Jefferson High School: 50% Construction Documents (CDs) were submitted on July 17, 2026,
      triggering CM/GC and third-party cost estimates. Early site and athletic field work Notice to
      Proceed (NTP) was issued June 15, 2026, and contractors have mobilized on site.
   ●​ Cleveland High School: 50% CDs are under review, with 100% CDs due late summer 2026.
      Early site demolition work has commenced. Certified sub-consultant participation remains strong
      on the architectural side.
