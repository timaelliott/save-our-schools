Attendance

Call to Order / Introductions

Director Englesman called the meeting to order at 4:35 pm.

    ●​ Holly Hofkamp - summarized themes from the American Psychological Association's recent

Discussion: 2026-27 Committee Work Plan

Director Englesman and Emily Courtage introduced the proposed 2026-27 committee work plan,
developed by the Chair and legal staff and noted the plan may shift as topics take more or less time than
expected. Revisions to Board Policy 3.30.080-P were posted early for review and will be discussed in
October, with public comment time provided. Committee members expressed appreciation for the work
plan.

Discussion: Schools Beyond Screens Digital Device and Screen Use Policy Draft

Director Englesman introduced the draft policy, which was developed by Schools Beyond Screens
Portland and received by legal and staff only the prior week. She noted that this was an initial discussion
only and that no presentation or staff analysis was expected until October, when staff will be asked to
weigh in on issues such as required assessments, digital resource purchasing and contracting, and
special education requirements. Committee members expressed strong interest in adopting a screen time
policy this year, and discussed a parent's concern about a school-issued laptop sent home with a
freshman without the ability to restrict its use.

Chair Wong recommended separating the draft into high-level direction, vision, and limits suited for policy
and prescriptive requirements suited for an administrative directive, so the District can adjust quickly to
unintended consequences, and recommended forming a teacher work group to identify any conflicts with
academic freedom and other policies. Members noted Schools Beyond Screens has been working with
the Portland Association of Teachers (PAT), and asked the group to document which teachers and PAT
representatives it has met with and what was said. Members also discussed unwinding existing screen
use in classrooms, limits on out-of-class screen time for grades 9-12, and giving families advance notice
of what technology will come home. Director Englesman asked staff for an answer by October on whether
Governor Kotek's assessment requirements apply to K-1 students, and Ms. Courtage agreed to follow up.
The item was placed on the October agenda.

Discussion: Artificial Intelligence (AI) Policy

Director Englesman started a preliminary discussion on developing an AI policy, noting that the District
has an AI guidebook and AI language in some administrative directives but no AI policy. Director La Forte
shared that the AI audit requested in June is expected in October and may be presented at either the
Teaching, Learning, and Enrollment Committee or the full Board. Committee members shared
observations that high school educators are restricting computer note-taking because of AI use and that
student feedback on AI has been overwhelmingly negative. Director Englesman asked for staff assistance
in preparing early policy ideas for October, and Committee members expressed interest in involving
educators.

Other Business and Future Agenda Items

Committee members suggested several future topics:

    ●​ Motorized bikes on school property: A community member raised safety concerns about
       motorized and electric bikes at schools. Members noted existing rules and limited enforcement
       authority and suggested staff review current policies and directives, and potentially involve the
       city or county.
    ●​ Outstanding bond audit recommendations: Director La Forte will send the Committee a list of
       outstanding recommendations from prior bond audits that relate to policy, including the Equity in
       Public Purchasing and Contracting policy, to be incorporated into the work plan.
    ●​ Vegetarian and plant-based meal options: Director Englesman proposed revising existing policies
       to ensure a vegetarian option in school meals, potentially through a nutrition policy, the Climate
       Crisis Response policy, or Board Policy 4.30.023.
   ●​ Use of student images: Director Englesman proposed a policy on the use of student images on
      social media and other public channels, tentatively scheduled for February. The Committee asked
      to hear from communications staff on the existing photo opt-out process and enforcement.
   ●​ Alternate transportation: Possibly addressed through the Climate Justice policy.

Adjourn

Director Engelsman adjourned the meeting at 5:28 pm.
