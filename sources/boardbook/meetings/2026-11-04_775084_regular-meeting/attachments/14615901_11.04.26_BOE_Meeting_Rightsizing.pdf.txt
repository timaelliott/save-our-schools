  What we heard – Naming the tensions


Prioritizing neighborhood schools vs. offering focus options
 ● Surveys say that the proposed scenarios over-emphasize choice programs
 ● Families that attend focus option programs highly value specialized models

Small neighborhood schools vs. consolidated programming
 ● Some K-8 families celebrating additional elective, sports, and academic tracks in larger middle schools
 ● Others mourn the loss of small school environments

Union seniority rules vs. retaining teachers of color
 ● Questions about how staffing will change during consolidations/closures
 ● Families of color raise concerns about losing teachers of color

Equity on the East side vs. geographic isolation on the west side
 ● Black, Native, and Latino/a/e families in North, Northeast, and Southeast emphasize that historically marginalized
     communities face a disproportionate share of school closures, boundary shifts, and community disruption.
 ● Westside families concerned about topography creating hazards for student safety
