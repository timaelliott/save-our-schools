BACKGROUND

As needed when positions become available, the Board of Education (Board) appoints members
to the Community Budget Review Committee (CBRC), to assist in the annual budget process.
The CBRC reviews, evaluates, and makes recommendations regarding the Superintendent's
Proposed Budget and other budgetary issues identified by the Board. The CBRC also monitors
and advises the Board on the allocation and expenditure of Local Option Levy funds.

The CBRC is currently composed of eight to thirteen volunteer members appointed by the Board
and typically meets on a monthly basis during the course of the budget development season.
There are also student representatives.

Applications to join the CBRC were accepted through September 27, 2026. Staff received four
applications, none of whom were students.

Additionally, staff have submitted recommendations for CBRC appointees. The CBRC currently
has four three-year positions available. Adoption of the appointment resolution would allow four
adult appointees to join the committee.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT (IF APPLICABLE)

In June of each year, the number of vacant positions is reviewed and confirmed. Vacancies are
posted on the CBRC webpage soliciting membership for the appropriate number of positions.
The application process was open from July through the end of August; the process including
timeline and criteria are transparent to the community and Board.

TIMELINE FOR IMPLEMENTATION / EVALUATION

To provide incoming members adequate time for onboarding and comprehensive review, we
recommend the Board finalize appointments at its earliest opportunity.

CONNECTION TO BOARD GOALS

A primary goal of the CBRC is to review the budget through the lens of making progress on the
Board’s goals.
