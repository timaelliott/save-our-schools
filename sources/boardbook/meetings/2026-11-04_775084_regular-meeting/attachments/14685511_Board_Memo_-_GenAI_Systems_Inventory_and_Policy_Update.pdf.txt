Executive Summary
This memorandum serves as the districtwide assessment requested by the Board of Education
on June 23, 2026 via the Budget Amendment “AI Pause REVISED” within Resolution 7326.

As directed, this response documents the approved applications and software systems currently
in operation utilizing GenAI across central office and instructional environments, establishes
updated governance standards, and formalizes our recommendations for future digital literacy
and technology use.


Definitions
  ●​ Artificial Intelligence (AI): The capability of computer systems or algorithms to imitate
     intelligent human behavior.
  ●​ Generative AI (GenAI): Artificial intelligence that is capable of generating new content
     (such as images or text) in response to a submitted prompt by learning from a large
     reference database of examples.
  ●​ Agentic AI: Able to accomplish results with autonomy, used especially in reference to
     artificial intelligence.

Promoting Balanced Instruction & Healthy Tech Use
I.   Definitions

            A. Screen Time: Screen Time refers to the amount of time a student is expected to
            view or interact with Device displays, including structured educational technology
            used for instruction and unstructured or non-instructional media. Screen Time does
            not include use of assistive technology required to provide a student with access to
            instruction or PPS services.

            B. Device: As used in this policy, a Device means any PPS-owned, PPS-issued, or
            PPS-managed laptop, desktop computer, tablet, mobile phone, wearable device,
            virtual or augmented reality equipment, or other internet-connected hardware used to
            access PPS systems or instructional content.

            C. Educational Technology: Educational Technology means software, digital
            applications, online services, websites, platforms, and other digital tools used for
            instructional, assessment, accessibility, safety, communication, or administrative
            purposes.

            D. Approved Educational Technology: Approved Educational Technology means
            Educational Technology that PPS has reviewed and authorized under its educational
            technology approval process.

II. Objective

         PPS will use educational technology when it supports a defined instructional,
         accessibility, safety, or operational purpose. Educators should select instructional
         methods best suited to the learning objective and provide developmentally appropriate
         opportunities for discussion, handwriting, reading in print, physical activity, collaborative
         work, creative expression, independent problem-solving, and other screen-free learning.
         Educational Technology should support, rather than routinely replace, effective
         educator-led instruction and interpersonal learning.

III. ​   Grade-Level Technology Restrictions

            A. Pre-K Through 8th Grade

                     i. PPS will limit use of PPS-issued Devices during instructional time in
            accordance with the PPS Screen Time Guidelines for PPS adopted curriculum and
            assessments:
          Pre-Kindergarten-Grade 2: Up to 130 minutes per week, inclusive of core instruction
          and intervention

          Grades 3-5: Up to 225 minutes per week inclusive of core instruction and intervention

          Grades 6-8: Up to 300 minutes per week across all classes

                    ii. Devices will be used to support specific in-class curriculum and
          learning activities and will not serve the primary instructional medium. PPS will not
          operate a standard one-to-one take-home Device program for students in Pre-K
          through Grade 8.

                     iii. Students may take PPS Devices home only to address an identified
          instructional, accessibility, equity, or academic-support need, as approved by the
          principal or designee, or as required by an individualized education program (“IEP”),
          Section 504 plan, or applicable law. PPS will provide the student’s parent or guardian
          with notice of the take-home arrangement and obtain an acknowledgment of
          applicable Device-use requirements. Failure to return an acknowledgment will not
          prevent PPS from providing a Device when the Device is required to provide the
          student with equal access, appropriate services, or legally required accommodations.

        B. High School

                     i. PPS will limit use of PPS-issued Devices during instructional time in
          accordance with PPS Screen Time Guidelines. Use must support a defined
          instructional, assessment, accessibility, safety, or operational purpose. Educators
          should incorporate appropriate screen-free instruction and activities based on the
          learning objective and students’ developmental needs.

                ii.   ​ PPS Devices will be issued to each student.


IV. ​   Content and Platforms Across All Grade Levels

          A. Streaming Restrictions: Classroom access to streaming media is limited to
          content that supports an instructional purpose and is selected by an educator through
          an Approved Educational Technology service. PPS will use industry-standard
          filtering and monitoring tools to block restricted content across platforms.

          Staff may not require or direct students to create accounts, disclose personal
          information, upload student work, or use an educational technology service unless the
          service has been approved through the PPS educational technology review process.
          Staff may not accept click-through terms on behalf of PPS or use free applications,
          browser extensions, pilot programs, or artificial intelligence tools unless authorized
          under that process.
           B. Responsible Use Policy: Consistent with the Responsible Use Policy, use of PPS
           Devices, accounts, networks, and systems may be monitored for legitimate
           educational, safety, security, legal, and operational purposes. Automated alerts or
           classifications will receive appropriate human review before being used for discipline,
           grading, special-education decisions, or other material decisions affecting a student.
           Students using PPS Devices, accounts, or networks will have no expectation of
           privacy.

V. ​     Operational Accountability

           A. EdTech Audit: PPS will conduct an annual review of all educational technology
           contracts and, to the extent feasible, conduct an assessment of unapproved digital
           technology use and report to the Board the results of the review.

           B. Evaluation of Impact of Technology Application Use: PPS will develop a plan
           for the adoption of digital technology used for instruction.

   VI.   Digital Literacy

   PPS will integrate developmentally appropriate Digital Literacy instruction into the core
   curriculum for grades K–12. This instruction will focus on:

           A. Evaluating source credibility and detecting misinformation;

           B. Understanding data privacy and the Responsible Use Policy;

           C. The environmental and social impacts of digital technologies;

           D. Safe and respectful use of digital tools;

           E. Skills to critically evaluate digital content;

           F. Developing critical thinking, creativity, communication, collaboration, empathy,
           judgment, and independent problem-solving skills in a rapidly evolving technological
           world; and

           G. Maintaining healthy technology habits, including attention management, periodic
           breaks, ergonomics, and awareness of persuasive or engagement-focused design.

   VI.   Professional Learning

   All PPS staff members will complete annual training about data privacy, security, and
   responsible use to ensure alignment with this policy, the Children’s Online Privacy Protect Act
   (COPPA), and the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA).
  VII.​ Exceptions

   The restrictions and limits provided in Section III of this Administrative Directive and the PPS
   Screentime Guidelines may be modified to be consistent with students’ IEPs or Section 504
   Plans based on individual students’ disability-related needs as determined by their IEP or
   504 teams.


Recommendations for Future Implementation
To ensure PPS remains secure, compliant, and educationally effective, we formally recommend
the following strategic initiatives:

  1.​ Instructional Staff Professional Development: Cross-functional collaboration between
      OTIS and Academic teams to design and deliver targeted professional learning. This will
      focus on literacy, critical thinking, data privacy, and instructional strategies to prepare staff
      for evolving technological realities. This effort requires funds which need to be sustained
      over multiple fiscal years.

  2.​ Development and Implementation of Digital Literacy: Student-facing digital literacy
      lessons that include critical evaluation of technological outputs, ethical use, and
      understanding bias.

  3.​ Back-Office Modernization: Continue leveraging non-student-interactive AI within
      approved back-office infrastructure (e.g., cybersecurity, Workday platform evolution,
      filtering). Restricting administrative modernizations poses a severe risk of reduced central
      services and unsustainable organic staff growth.

  4.​ Parent & Community Engagement: Deepen partnerships with parent and family
      advocacy groups (such as Schools Beyond Screens) to amplify shared goals surrounding
      digital literacy and healthy technology usage outside the traditional school day.

  5.​ Regular Updates to Board, Administrative Directives and AI Guidebook: Brief the
      Board three times a year on the current state and future roadmap of district technology,
      updating PPS Administrative Directives and the AI Guidebook on the same schedule.
