                         Intergovernmental Affairs Committee Report


From: ​Christy Splitt, Committee Chair
To: Board of Education

Meeting Dates: October 13, 2026 and October 17, 2026


This is my first update from the Intergovernmental Affairs Committee, so this is a report out on
our work thus far this fall. Our primary aim is to develop the legislative agenda for the district. To
that end, the committee received from staff, vetted, and recommended to the full board the
Legislative Principles that the full board has since adopted. We also received a really helpful
training from the Oregon School Board Association on the big issues we should all know about
ahead of session; I highly recommend all board members watch that presentation. Since then,
we have heard from other entities that we work closely with about their planned legislative
priorities: Portland Association of Teachers (who shared the Oregon Education Association
priorities), Community and Parents for Public Schools, Multnomah Education Services District,
Northwest Regional Education Services District, and Salem-Keizer Public Schools. At our next
meeting, we will review and recommend to the board a legislative agenda based on the adopted
principles and all that we have learned in committee meetings thus far. This proposed legislative
agenda will be in front of the board on November 17. From then on, Intergovernmental Affairs
Committee meetings will happen as needed and will focus on organizing our work as a board
this legislative session, which is an especially critical one for our schools, staff, and students.

Next Meeting: November 10, 2026.
