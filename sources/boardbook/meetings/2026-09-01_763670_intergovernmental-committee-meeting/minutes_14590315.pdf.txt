                  Intergovernmental Affairs Committee Meeting


                                      Informal Meeting Minutes

In accordance with ORS 192.650, the District’s official School Board Meeting Minutes are maintained via
video recording and may be viewed at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation

Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, a Portland Public Schools Board of Education Intergovernmental Affairs Committee meeting
was held virtually and streamed live at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

Attendees

Committee Members: Christy Splitt (Committee Chair); Patte Sullivan; Stephanie Engelsman​
Other attendees: Michelle Morrison - Chief Financial Officer; Ben Gurewitz - Senior Manager,
Government Relations; Deborah Kafoury - Chief of Staff; Rosanne Powell - Senior Manager, Board
Operations.

Call to Order / Introductions

Committee Chair Splitt called the meeting to order at 12:02 pm.

Public Comments

There was no public comment.

Committee Purpose and Overview of the Year Ahead

Staff provided an overview of the purpose and planned work of the committee in preparation for the 2027
legislative session. The committee will work toward developing legislative priorities for Board
consideration, hear from external partners about their legislative priorities, and provide opportunities for
Board members to receive legislative updates and discuss advocacy strategies. There was a discussion
on the timeline to develop a legislative agenda for Board adoption before the start of the long session.

Public Employee Retirement System (PERS) Update and Overview

Chief Financial Officer Michelle Morrison provided an overview of the Public Employee Retirement
System (PERS). She shared the financial impact of upcoming changes to the PERS employer rate, with
the greatest impact expected in 2027–28. More information is coming in October.
Committee members asked questions about pension obligation bonds, side accounts, employee eligibility
and vesting, and the potential for future strategies to address PERS costs. Chief Morrison noted that staff
is exploring options and expects to bring additional information to the Board. The committee agreed that
PERS is a complex topic that warrants continued discussion.

Review and Discussion of Legislative Principles

Staff presented proposed legislative principles to help guide the development of the Board’s 2027
legislative priorities and support Board members in conversations with legislators. The proposed
principles included adequate funding to meet student needs, support for students with disabilities,
strengthening educator preparation programs and ending unfunded mandates. Committee members also
expressed the importance of raising the issue of the financial impact of PERS to school districts.

Committee members requested a list of unfunded mandates, including information about associated costs
and other impacts on the District. Staff noted that the Coalition of School Administrators and Oregon
School Boards Association have an updated list that includes some cost information.

The committee discussed the role of external partners in developing the legislative priorities.
Organizations invited to upcoming meetings include the Portland Association of Teachers, Community
and Parents for Public Schools. Committee members were invited to suggest additional organizations.

Future Topics / Next Steps

Committee members expressed interest in additional guidance on effective legislative advocacy and how
Board members and community members can engage with legislators. The committee will continue its
discussion of the legislative principles at the next meeting and consider a recommendation to the Board.
The committee also plans to continue the PERS discussion, including an opportunity for Chief Morrison to
return for follow-up questions.

Adjourn

Committee Chair Splitt adjourned the meeting at 1:00 pm.
