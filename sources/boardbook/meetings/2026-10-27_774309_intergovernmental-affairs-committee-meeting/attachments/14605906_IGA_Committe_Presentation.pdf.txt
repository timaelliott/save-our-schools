                   How We Will Review Legislation
Every bill should strengthen a coherent, equitable, and eﬀective education system.

 ●   System alignment: Does it connect statewide goals, policies, and investments while clarifying
     responsibilities?
 ●   Student opportunity: Does it improve instruction, expand access to support, and close
     opportunity gaps?
 ●   Adequate resources: Are full implementation and ongoing costs identiﬁed and sustainably
     funded?
 ●   Capacity to deliver: Are staﬃng, guidance, training, and timelines suﬃcient and realistic?
 ●   Accountability and improvement: Are outcomes measurable, data reliable, and supports in
     place to improve results?

Support bills that advance these goals. Seek amendments to improve alignment and
implementation. Oppose unfunded obligations, fragmentation, and duplication without credible
student beneﬁt.

    Administrators from across the state on March 18 at the Oregon Capitol.

    November with resources families can use and engage with on their own time.

           Legislative Agenda – Priority 1

•Modernize Oregon's K-12 Funding System
  • The formula no longer reﬂects the current educational needs
    of students or the operational realities of the districts.

•Proposed Policy Statement(s)
  • Create a statewide School Funding Modernization Commission
  • Robust stakeholder engagement + expert input
  • Operate under deﬁned timelines and deliverables
  • Update the state's measure and method for educational adequacy
    and distribution formula.
         Legislative Priority –2

•Preserve Current Service Level investment
  •CSL – biennium funding level determined by ODE that
   is necessary to preserve current educational programs
   and services.
  •If CSL is not fully funded, SKPS will need to make
   deeper budget reductions.
          Legislative Priority - 3

•Addressing cost pressures outside district control
  • Negatively impacts long term ﬁnancial planning and
    maximizing investments in student success.

•Proposed Policy Statement
  • Urge the legislature to address cost pressures outside
    district control through policy reforms and funding
    mechanisms that mitigate or cover these costs, with an
    initial focus on the Unemployment Insurance.
           Legislative Priority - 4

•Strengthen early learning and school readiness system
  • Early learning and preschool programs help reduce
    achievement gaps.

•Proposed Policy Statement
  • Call upon the legislature to make strategic investments that
    strengthen OR's early learning and school readiness system,
    including EI/ECSE and family engagement programs.
            Legislative Priority - 5

•Restore meaningful participation in state assessments
  • Low participation rates can limit the district's ability to analyze
    program effectiveness and identify student needs.

•Proposed Policy Statement
  • Support legislative changes that strengthens participation in
    statewide assessments. This will ensure Oregon's
    accountability system is built on reliable and representative
    student data.
             Legislative Priority - 6

•Streamline administrative and compliance requirements
   •Increased requirements without corresponding resources to
    support implementation.
•Proposed Policy Statement
  • Urge legislature to streamline grant administration, reporting
    requirements, and compliance processes.
  • Key focus on Complaint Process Reform – consolidate overlapping
    complaints systems and reduce duplicative investigations.
           Legislative Priority - 7

•Virtual charter schools- accountability and oversight
  • Growing enrollment in virtual charters without a consistent
    authorization or renewal standards.

•Proposed Policy Statement
  • Support legislation that supports a consistent statewide
    framework for virtual charter school authorization and
    renewal to ensure that virtual charters are accountable for
    student outcomes and for responsible use of public funds.
                   Broad policy positions consistent with
                   district's values and mission
The Board supports:
• High-quality instructional materials and expanded instructional time and urges for adequate and
  sustainable funding to support implementation.

• Statewide strategies that improve student attendance and engagement.

• Policies that allow maintaining local ﬂexibility over stafﬁng allocation and opposes making class size as a
  mandatory subject of collective bargaining.

• Thoughtful and intentional use of technology when it advances instructional and academic goals. Opposes
  broad restrictions or complete ban.

• Expansion of Medicaid reimbursement opportunities for school-based student support services, including
  community health workers and peer support specialists.

• Legislative actions that provides meaningful PERS cost relief for districts while protecting retirement
  commitments to current and retired employees.
