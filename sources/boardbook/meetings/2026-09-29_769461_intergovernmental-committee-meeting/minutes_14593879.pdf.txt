                   Intergovernmental Affairs Committee Meeting


                                           Informal Meeting Minutes

In accordance with ORS 192.650, the District's official School Board Meeting Minutes are maintained via
video recording and may be viewed at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation

Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board's public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, a Portland Public Schools Board of Education Intergovernmental Affairs Committee meeting
was held virtually and streamed live at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

Attendees

Committee Members: Christy Splitt (Committee Chair); Patte Sullivan; Stephanie Englesman

Other attendees: Deborah Kafoury - Chief of Staff; Stacy Michaelson - Director of Government Relations
and Communications, Oregon School Boards Association (OSBA); Lesley Rivers - Government Relations
and Member Engagement Specialist, OSBA; Rosanne Powell - Senior Board Manager.

Call to Order / Introductions

Director Engelsman called the meeting to order at 12:08 pm.

Public Comments

Michael Tynan - Cleveland High School parent, shared concerns about student transportation on TriMet
going to and from the Marshall campus, including issues with students being prevented from boarding if
they did not have school ID’s or fare.

Committee members agreed the issue should be added to the October 6 full Board agenda, and asked
that TriMet be invited to attend and provide answers.

OSBA Legislative Process and School Funding Overview

Stacy Michaelson and Lesley Rivers of OSBA provided an overview of the legislative process and the
K-12 funding landscape to help the Committee prepare for its advocacy work. They encouraged Board
members to build relationships with legislators early, to tell the story of their district, to be mindful of when
they are speaking on behalf of the Board versus as an individual, and to stay in touch throughout the
session.

Committee members asked questions about the caps and regrowth of the Education Stability Fund and
Rainy Day Fund, whether the QEM includes PERS costs, and whether the Education Stability Fund could
be used to support PERS.
Legislator Outreach

Director Splitt shared legislative representation by board zones, and suggested Board members might
meet with which legislators based on where they live. She also explained the Board plans to meet with
legislators before the session to hear their priorities, discuss the District's work, and share the Board's
draft legislative principles.

Future Topics / Next Steps

The Committee will meet again in two weeks to hear from external partners on their legislative priorities.
Stand for Children was invited at Director Englesman's request but declined; the other groups discussed
previously will present over the next several meetings.

Adjourn

Director Splitt adjourned the meeting at 1:01 pm.
