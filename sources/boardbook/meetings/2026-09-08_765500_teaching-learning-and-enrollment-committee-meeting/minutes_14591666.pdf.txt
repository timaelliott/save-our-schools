Update on Rightsizing: Well Resourced Schools

Presenters: Kristina Howard - Senior Chief of Schools - Senior Director, PK-12 Core Academics, Emily
Glasgow - Filip Hristic - Senior Director, K-12 Programs and Pathways

Staff presented an update on the District's right sizing work, including the working definition of a
well-resourced school. Committee members asked about whether the process would include absolutes
(such as eliminating K-8s or split feeders), how pathways to bolster Roosevelt's enrollment are being
considered, and why boundary changes alone cannot balance enrollment. There were also questions
about impacts on staff, student travel safety, enrollment and whether any elementary schools are well
resourced. There was discussion of K-8 versus middle school performance and student experience data,
as well as questions about target enrollment thresholds.
There was discussion about blended classrooms, middle school electives, the ability to have full staff like
counselors. Staff stated that a fiscal impact analysis would be provided once scenarios are developed
and a recommendation is made. Chair La Forte stressed the importance of sharing specifics before
families make decisions about the next school year in January. Staff shared that they are looking at
seismic and facility conditions when making recommendations.

Upcoming TLE Agenda Topics

Committee Chair La Forte previewed upcoming agenda topics, including a curriculum adoption deep dive,
follow up on the Artificial Intelligence (AI) audit, middle school academic rigor and student outcomes,
monthly spotlights on high school programming, including AP, IB, dual credit, and CTE offerings and
continued updates on the district continuous improvement plan.There is a planned Science of Reading
update in November and as well as a presentation on the Division 22 exemptions.​


Other Business and Future Agenda Items

There was a request for regular updates on Jefferson High School course offerings, which could be
incorporated into the AP, IB, and dual credit discussion. Members also requested that future presentations
include more teacher and student voices, and asked whether time with union partners could be structured
as a Q&A.

Adjourn

Committee Chair La Forte adjourned the meeting at 5:52 pm.
