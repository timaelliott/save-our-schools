Assessing Facilities Readiness Process
 ●   When initially assessing a school’s readiness to welcome students we looked at a
     building’s functional capacity, the current utilization rate, and the number of work orders
     received from each building.

 ●   We utilized our data from our facilities teams, and pulled data from Tririga, which shows
     the number of classrooms currently being used for what purpose.

 ●   In June, we brought principals in to look at their school’s floor plan and provide feedback
     on how they are using their spaces.

 ●   When scenarios were released, operations teams comprised of facilities, maintenance,
     RISE, safety and security, nutrition, and transportation did in-person walkthroughs with
     principals of 21 buildings set to experience the highest number of student increase.

District-Wide ADA Accessibility
● Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) requires state and local
  governments, including public school districts, to give people with
  disabilities an equal opportunity to benefit from all of their programs,
  services, and activities.
● School districts must provide program access by ensuring that students
  with disabilities are not excluded from programs because existing buildings
  or facilities are inaccessible to them.
● When school districts build or alter facilities, they must make them
  accessible to people with disabilities, following ADA standards.
● All PPS campuses are ADA accessible to the main floor.
