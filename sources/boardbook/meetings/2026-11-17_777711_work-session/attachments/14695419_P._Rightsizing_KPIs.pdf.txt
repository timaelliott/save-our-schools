Overview

These measures come from the guiding principles adopted by the school board in May of
2026 and factors outlined board policies.

We use these key performance indicators to compare scenarios to each other as well as to
the current state if we make no changes. They will help surface tradeoffs between guiding
principles and factors outlined in the policies.

Guiding Principle Description

PPS is committed to students learning in environments that support their physical safety,
health, accessibility, well-being, and ability to learn.


As part of Rightsizing, PPS will assess multiple factors together to understand how each
scenario affects students' access to safe and healthy learning environments. The primary
KPIs for this guiding principle are “Right Use of Space” and “Right Boundaries”. “Right
Use of Space” is measured by increasing the percentage of schools that are efficiently
utilized, defined as operating between 70% and 100% total functional utilization capacity.
“Right Boundaries” and walkability is measured by the percentage of students who live
within a one and one-and-a-half mile radius of the school.


Building condition, accessibility, safety, and educational suitability data will serve as internal
assessment factors that inform scenario development, identify trade-offs and highlight
potential investments or mitigations, but they will not be the primary driver of scenario
selection. No single facility indicator determines whether a school is “safe” or “unsafe,” and
no single facility measure will be the primary determining factor in a scenario.

How We Will Use These Measures

For each Rightsizing scenario, PPS will examine multiple indicators collectively to determine
whether the proposed configuration provides stronger, comparable, or more challenging
conditions for students.

The analysis will consider:
    Utilization rates and functional capacity for a building;
    Existing conditions at both sending and receiving schools;
    The number of students affected by changes in those conditions;
    Trade-offs across indicators rather than relying on a single indicator or data point;
    Improvements or mitigation needed for a receiving school to successfully serve additional
    students; and
    Equity impacts across all indicators, including whether particular student groups experience
    disproportionate benefits or burdens.

PPS will consider current facility conditions, the extent to which those conditions affect safe and
reliable school operations, and any investments or mitigations needed to ensure a receiving
school can successfully support students. Immediate health, safety, or regulatory concerns will
continue to be addressed through PPS’s established maintenance, environmental health, risk
management, and capital planning processes independent of Rightsizing decisions.


     GUIDING QUESTION

     "To what extent does this scenario improve students' access to educationally
     supportive, safe, healthy, and accessible learning environments?"
     “…and what risks or investments would need to be addressed through implementation?”
