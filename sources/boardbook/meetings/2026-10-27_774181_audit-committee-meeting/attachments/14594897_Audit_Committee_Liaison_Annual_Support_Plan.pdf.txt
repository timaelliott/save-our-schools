                 Audit Committee Liaison Annual Support Plan

The CFO will serve as the staff Liaison for the Audit Committee with support from the Board
Manager. The Internal Performance Auditor will serve as a resource to the committee with a
standing agenda item for Audit Work Plan updates and other items as needed.

   ●​   Audit Committee Members
   ●​   Board Member Committee Chair
   ●​   Chief Financial Officer
   ●​   Internal Performance Auditor
   ●​   Legal Support (all board meetings)
   ●​   Board Manager for agenda setting and tracking
   ●​   Designee for facilitation (in-person and online options, livestream)

Sample Annual Plan (additional ad-hoc meetings can be scheduled with notice)


        Month                                             Topics

 October-December        Orientation, Annual OIPA Work Plan Review and Updates, Other Audit
                         Activities and Current Topics

 January-February        ACFR Preview (prior to Board Adoption), OIPA Work Plan Updates,
                         Other Audit Activities and Current Topics

 March-May               OIPA Work Plan Updates, Other Audit Activities and Current Topics

 June                    Budget changes (if needed), OIPA Work Plan Updates, Other Audit
                         Activities and Current Topics
