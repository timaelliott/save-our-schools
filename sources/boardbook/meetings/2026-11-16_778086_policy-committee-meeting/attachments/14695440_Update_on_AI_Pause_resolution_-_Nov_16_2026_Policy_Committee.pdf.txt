                                   AI Pause Directive #5d

Recommendations regarding future implementation, limitations,
 or expansion of GenAI technologies within PPS

   Knowledge of AI should be treated similar to a required language
   PPS should integrate developmentally appropriate Digital Literacy instruction into the core curriculum for
   grades K–12, starting in the 2027-28 school year

   ◆ Evaluating source credibility and detecting misinformation
   ◆ Understanding data privacy and responsible use
   ◆ The environmental and social impacts of digital technologies
   ◆ Safe and respectful use of digital tools, including social media
   ◆ Skills to critically evaluate digital content
   ◆ Developing critical thinking, creativity, communication, collaboration, empathy, judgment, and
     independent problem-solving skills in a rapidly evolving technological world
   ◆ Maintaining healthy technology habits, including attention management, periodic breaks, ergonomics,
     and awareness of persuasive or engagement-focused design.
