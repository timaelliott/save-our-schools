​IV.​   ​Filing a Division 22​​Formal​​Complaint – Step 1​

           ​A.​​The written complaint must be filed with​​the District​​PPS​​’s​​c​C​omplaint​​c​C​oordinator by​
                ​letter, email, or th​​e​​PPS Unified Complaint Form​​written complaint​​form​​.​​The written​
                 ​complaint should include the name and contact information for the​​c​C​omplainant, a​
                  ​description of the concern, and the student’s name, if applicable. It is helpful if the​
                   ​written complaint also includes the names of any other parties involved, including​
                    ​witnesses; a description of efforts to resolve the concern; and suggestions for​
                     ​resolution. The​​c​C​omplainant​​will​​shall​​receive a written acknowledgement of receipt​

           ​B.​​All Division 22​​Formal C​​c​omplaints processed under this policy will receive a decision​
                ​in writing that addresses each concern raised and contains reasons for​​the​
                 ​District​​PPS​​’s decision within 30 days of receipt of the complaint, unless the parties​
                  ​agree to extend the deadline, and the decision will include information about next​
                   ​steps in the complaint process.​

​V.​    ​ iling an Appeal of a Division 22​​Formal​​Complaint Decision – Step 2 Appeal to the​
        F
        ​PPS​​School Board​

           ​A. If a​​Formal C​​c​omplaint about a Division 22 matter is not fully substantiated, the​
               ​c​C​omplainant may request a review of the unsubstantiated allegations by the​​PPS​
                ​School Board. The request for review​​shall​​will​​be submitted in writing within 10 days​
                 ​of the​​c​C​omplainant receiving the decision in Step 1.​

           ​B. The Board will vote on the appeal within 30 days of the written request to appeal​​the​
               ​District​​PPS​​’s Step 1 decision unless the parties agree to a longer time period. The​
                ​Board will have the full written record of the decisions at Step 1. The​​c​C​omplainant​
                 ​shall​​will​​also have the full written record and any materials or information provided to​
                  ​Board members before the hearing about the complaint unless otherwise protected​
                   ​from disclosure by law.​

           ​C.​​District​​PPS​​staff and the​​c​C​omplainant​​shall​​will​​exchange any other written​
                ​background information they intend to present to the Board at the appeal hearing at​
                 ​least 24 hours before the hearing. If new information is offered less than 24 hours​
                  ​before the Board hearing, the​​c​C​omplainant may ask for an extension of time.​

           ​D.​​Complainants may bring an advocate or support person to any meeting or​
                ​proceeding. For hearings before the Board,​​the District​​PPS​​will inform the​

           ​E.​​Because the Board makes the final decision at the Step​​2​1​​appeal, Board members​

​VI.​   ​Other Provisions​

           ​A.​​The District​​PPS​​will provide translation and interpretation services and other​
                ​reasonable accommodations to assist​​c​C​omplainants. Legal advice is not a resource​
                 ​provided by​​the District​​PPS​​.​

           ​B.​​The District​​The PPS​​representative assigning staff to investigate and respond to a​
                ​complaint​​shall​​will​​avoid any conflicts of interest or the appearance of conflicts of​
                 ​interest.​

           ​C.​​Retaliation against any person who files or participates in a​​District​​PPS​​complaint​
                ​process is strictly prohibited. Retaliation is any intentional action that would deter a​
                 ​reasonable person from participating in the process. Anyone who believes they have​
                  ​suffered retaliation should immediately report it to the Superintendent or​​PPS​​School​
                   ​Board​​of Directors​​.​

           ​D.​​The District​​PPS​​will share with​​c​C​omplainants as much information as possible about​
                ​the findings of the investigation and will, in all cases, share the outcome of its​
                                                          ​4​
                  i​nvestigation of complaints. However, PPS is often prohibited from disclosing specific​
                   ​information about disciplinary action taken against an employee or student involved in​
                    ​the complaint​​or other legally protected information​​.​

              ​E.​​The Board will hear appeals of complaints that involve confidential student records in​
                   ​a session closed to the public and media unless the student or family requests the​
                    ​appeal be heard in a session open to the public.​

              ​F.​ ​As used in this policy, “days” will be counted as “calendar days.” Any period for​
                    ​response under this policy that falls on a weekend or legal holiday​​shall​​will​​be​
                     ​extended to the next business day.​

              ​G.​​The District​​PPS​​may not be able to assure confidentiality of the names of persons​
                   ​who file complaints under this policy.​

              ​H.​​If a complaint alleges employee misconduct that is outside the scope of this policy,​
                   ​the complaint coordinator will notify the Superintendent in writing of that filing, and​​the​
                    ​District​​PPS​​will endeavor to respond in a timely manner.​

              ​I.​ ​Current and former employees may not bring a complaint under this policy regarding​
                    ​the terms, conditions, or status of their employment.​

              ​J.​ ​Complaints that have been previously filed by a​​c​C​omplainant, investigated, and​
                    ​responded to and for which appeals have been exhausted or the time for appeal has​
                     ​expired, cannot be refiled when the complaint is regarding a particular incident or​
                      ​issue that has already been addressed.​
