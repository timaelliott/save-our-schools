​BACKGROUND AND ANALYSIS​

​ inor updates are needed to PPS’s Complaint Policy, 4.50.030-P, to comport with recent​
M
​guidance PPS received from Oregon Department of Education staff. Specifically, ODE staff​
 ​clarified that Division 22 complaints concern district-wide practices and do not include individual​
  ​complaints about discrimination or bias, restraint and seclusion, or retaliation. Rather, those​
   ​individual complaints fall under separate Oregon Administrative Rules or statutes. Accordingly,​
    ​this policy revision clarifies those district-wide or systemic matters that are Division 22​
     ​complaints (​​see​​Section II(C)(i)) subject to the Division​​22 complaint procedures herein vs all​
      ​other types of District complaints (​​see​​Section II(B)).​​The latter - non-Division 22 complaints -​
       ​are subject to specific complaint procedures governed by state or federal laws and/or by PPS​
        ​Administrative Directives.​​1​

​ econd, this revision removes the confusing use of the word “formal” to describe Division 22​
S
​complaints. We found that complainants frequently use the word “formal” to refer to written​
 ​complaints submitted through the Uniform Complaint Form, regardless of type of complaint. To​
  ​avoid confusion, we recommend removing the word “formal” as a descriptor for Division 22​
   ​complaints. This change has no effect on the complaint policy or the processing of Division 22​
    ​complaints or other types of complaints.​

​Third, we updated links, form names, contact information, and position titles.​

​ inally, we updated formatting and language usage (​​e.g.​​, using “PPS” instead of “District,” “will”​
F
​instead of “shall”) to ensure alignment with other recent policy and administrative directive​
 ​updates.​
