                                         Informal Meeting Minutes

In accordance with ORS 192.650, the District's official School Board Meeting Minutes are maintained via

Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board's public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, a Portland Public Schools Board of Education Intergovernmental Affairs Committee meeting
was held virtually and streamed live at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live




Attendees

Committee Members: Christy Splitt (Committee Chair); Patte Sullivan; Stephanie Englesman

Other attendees: Deborah Kafoury- Chief of Staff; Ben Gurewitz - Senior Manager, Government
Relations; Kimberly Gibson - Portland Public Schools; Rosanne Powell - Senior Manager, Board

Call to Order / Introductions

Director Splitt called the meeting to order at 12:04 pm.

Public Comments

There were none.

Portland Association of Teachers (PAT) Legislative Priorities

PAT Vice President Tina Lamanna, presented the Oregon Education Association's (OEA) legislative
priorities for the 2027 session, which PAT will support and explained that PAT does not set its own
legislative priorities.

Committee members discussed areas of alignment with the District's legislative principles, including
raising or removing the special education cap. Director Splitt suggested the Board consider whether to
take a position on increasing revenue and whether to add language on AI, edtech, and screen time to its
principles. Director Englesman emphasized that new mandates must come with funding and cautioned
that the 15% special education cap proposed was lower than the rate PPS currently serves and should be
set high enough to fund special education adequately. Director Sullivan asked that the Board guard
against unfunded mandates.
Community and Parents for Public Schools (CPPS) Legislative Priorities

Representatives of CPPS presented their organization's views on legislative priorities, and agreed with
the District's draft legislative principles. They urged the District to be more specific, including calling for
raising or eliminating the special education cap, providing the public with data on how the system is
serving English learners and students with disabilities, and publishing a list of unfunded mandates with
their fiscal impact. CPPS also shared a video explaining how Oregon school funding works, and
encouraged community members to contact their legislators.

Committee members discussed the effects of Measures 5 and 50 on school funding and ways to
communicate this to the public. They requested adding fiscal impacts to the unfunded mandates list to
help the community members wishing to advocate.

Future Topics / Next Steps

The next meeting is scheduled to hear from regional Education Service Districts and Salem-Keizer Public
Schools. Director Englesman requested outreach to local elected officials, including the Mayor,
Multnomah County leaders, city councilors, and commissioners, to build partnerships on education
funding.

Adjourn

Director Splitt adjourned the meeting at 12:58 pm.
