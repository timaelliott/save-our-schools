                                             Resolution No. 7397

    Resolution to Authorize Independent Fact-finding Regarding the Related to Use of Certain Non-PPS-
                                     approved Messaging Platforms


                                                  RECITALS


    A. The Board of Education has reviewed a news report alleging that some PPS employees have
       used the Signal messaging application to communicate about PPS business. Signal is not a
       PPS-approved platform for conducting official PPS communications.

    B. Use of non-approved messaging platforms by a PPS employee for some purposes would not
       necessarily violate PPS policy or state law.

    C. It is in the best interest of PPS to (1) understand whether, how, and why Signal was used by PPS
       employees; and (2) determine whether any Signal messages sent or received by PPS employees
       were public records and, if so, whether they are preserved within the application or can otherwise
       be recovered.

    D. In light of the proliferation of messaging applications, it is in the best interest of PPS for the PPS
       Board and administration to consider whether any changes to PPS policies and administrative
       guidance are advisable to provide guidance to PPS employees.



WHEREAS, the Portland Public Schools Board of Education (“Board”) has reviewed a Willamette Week
news report alleging that the Superintendent and Portland Public Schools (“District”) “leaders” used the
Signal messaging application to communicate about District business, and has also reviewed images of
Signal conversations with District leadership and individuals acting on behalf of the District that have been
made publicly available online. Signal is an encrypted messaging platform with a disappearing-messages
feature and is not a District-approved platform for official District communications;

WHEREAS, it is in the best interest of the District to independently determine whether, how, and why
Signal or other non-District-approved messaging platforms were used to conduct District business by the
Superintendent, current and/or former District leaders, and/or individuals or entities acting on behalf of the
District;

WHEREAS, the Board has an oversight responsibility to ensure compliance with state statutes, District
policies, public records requirements, and applicable records-retention laws;



                                                RESOLUTION


The Board of Education authorizes and directs the PPS General Counsel to work with outside legal
counsel to initiate fact-finding with the following scope and to provide the results of the fact-finding to the
Board:

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, the Board authorizes and directs the District to immediately
retain independent legal counsel to advise and represent the Board, and to initiate and manage fact-
finding with the following scope and to provide the results of the fact-finding directly and solely to the full
Board by December 11, 2026. District General Counsel and District personnel shall provide necessary
records, information, and logistical assistance but will not have authority to direct independent counsel or
the fact-finding process.


     1. Determine whether Signal or other similar applications are used by PPS management employees
        for matters relating to PPS, including the topics and purposes of such communications.

     2. Determine whether any PPS employees retained information that was transmitted through Signal
        or similar application and whether that information can be collected and preserved under
        applicable document retention standards.

     1. Such other matters as follow from the information gathered that relate to compliance with PPS
        policies and applicable law, or which would inform potential improvements to PPS policies or
        administrative guidance.


     1. Use of messaging platforms: Determine whether Signal or other non-District-approved
        messaging platforms were used to conduct District business by the Superintendent and/or District
        leadership employees, or entities acting on behalf of the District; identify the individuals involved,
        relevant time periods, and purposes of the communications.
     2. Direction or authorization: Determine whether the Superintendent and/or District leadership
        employees, or entities acting on behalf of the District directed employees to use non-PPS-
        approved messaging platforms to conduct District business.
     3. Records preservation: Determine whether communications constituted public records or were
        otherwise subject to District records-retention requirements, whether they were appropriately
        preserved, and whether deleted, disappearing, or otherwise unavailable communications can be
        recovered.
     4. Policy and legal compliance: Assess whether the practices identified were consistent with
        applicable statutes, District policies, administrative guidance, public records requirements, and
        records-retention obligations.

     BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the District shall immediately preserve all potentially relevant
     records and information, including electronic communications, attachments, screenshots, and
     metadata, and take reasonable steps to prevent further deletion or loss.

     BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that independent counsel will establish a confidential channel and
     method for direct communication through which current and former employees and others with
     relevant information may voluntarily provide information and/or documents.

     BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that independent counsel shall, at all times, report directly to the full
     Board and provide findings no later than December 11, 2026. If additional time is necessary,
     independent counsel shall provide an interim report that addresses the scope of fact-finding and
     proposed completion timeline by that date.
