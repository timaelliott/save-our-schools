                                      Resolution No. 7397

  Resolution to Authorize Independent Fact-Finding Regarding the Use of Non-PPS-Approved
                                    Messaging Platforms

                                            RECITALS

   A.​ WHEREAS, the Portland Public Schools Board of Education (“Board”) has reviewed a
       Willamette Week news report alleging that Portland Public Schools (“District”) “leaders”
       used the Signal messaging application to communicate about District business, and has
       also reviewed images of Signal conversations with District leadership and contractors or
       vendors that have been made publicly available online. Signal is an encrypted
       messaging platform with a disappearing-messages feature and is not a District-approved
       platform for official District communications;

   B.​ WHEREAS, it is in the best interest of the District to independently determine whether,
       how, and why Signal or other non-District-approved messaging platforms were used to
       conduct District business by current and/or former District leaders, and/or individuals or
       entities acting on behalf of the District;

   C.​ WHEREAS, the Board has an oversight responsibility to ensure compliance with state
       statutes, District policies, public records requirements, and applicable records-retention
       laws;

                                          RESOLUTION

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, the Board authorizes and directs the District to
immediately retain independent legal counsel to advise and represent the Board, and to
initiate and manage fact-finding with the following scope and to provide the results of
the fact-finding directly and solely to the full Board by December 11, 2026. District personnel
shall provide necessary records, information, and logistical assistance but will not have authority
to direct independent counsel or the fact-finding process.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the fact-finding scope shall include the following:

   1.​ Use of messaging platforms: Determine whether Signal or other non-PPS-approved
       messaging platforms were used to conduct District business by District leadership
       employees, or entities acting on behalf of the District; identify those individuals involved,
       relevant time periods, and purposes of the communications.

   2.​ Direction or authorization: Determine whether District leadership employees, or entities
       acting on behalf of the District directed employees to use non-PPS-approved messaging
       platforms to conduct District Business.
   3.​ Records preservation: Determine whether communications constituted public
       records or were otherwise subject to District records-retention requirements,
       whether they were appropriately preserved, and whether deleted, disappearing,
       or otherwise unavailable communications can be recovered.

   4.​ Policy and legal compliance: Assess whether the practices identified were
       consistent with applicable statutes, District policies, administrative guidance,
       public records requirements, and records-retention obligations.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the District shall immediately preserve all potentially
relevant records and information, including electronic communications, attachments,
screenshots, and metadata, and take reasonable steps to prevent further deletion or
Loss.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, the outside legal counsel will provide confidentiality to persons in
appropriate circumstances and to the extent possible within the bounds of the law, and will
inform persons providing information that they are protected by state law and District policy
barring retaliation against persons for providing this type of information.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that independent counsel shall, at all times, report
directly to the full Board and provide findings no later than December 11, 2026. If
additional time is necessary, independent counsel shall provide an interim report that
addresses the scope of fact-finding and proposed completion timeline by that date. The Board
will release a report on its fact-finding to the public following completion of the Board’s
fact-finding.
