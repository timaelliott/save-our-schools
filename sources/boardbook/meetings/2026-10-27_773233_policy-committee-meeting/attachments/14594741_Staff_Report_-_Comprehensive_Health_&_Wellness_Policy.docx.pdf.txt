BACKGROUND

Portland Public Schools is committed to creating safe and healthy learning environments that
support the academic, physical, social, and emotional development of every student. The
proposed revision to Board Policy 6.40.013, Comprehensive Health Education, establishes a
comprehensive Health & Wellness Policy that aligns district policy with the Whole School,
Whole Community, Whole Child (WSCC) framework.

The proposed policy recognizes that student health and academic success are interconnected and
that supporting student wellness requires coordination across instructional, operational, clinical,
and administrative functions. The policy therefore establishes a comprehensive framework
addressing School Food and Nutrition, Physical Education and Physical Activity, Comprehensive
Health Education, Healthy School Environments, Safe and Supportive Environments, Health
Services, and Employee Wellness.

The proposed policy also formally establishes a District Wellness Council, currently referred to
as a School Health Advisory Council (SHAC), to coordinate, monitor, and evaluate
implementation of the policy and an annual Wellness Action Plan1.

As part of this policy revision, staff recommends rescinding Board Policy 3.60.060-P, Student
Wellness through Nutrition and Physical Activity. The proposed Comprehensive Health &

ANALYSIS OF SITUATION

The proposed revision to Board Policy 6.40.013-P establishes a single, coherent policy
framework for student and staff health and wellness consistent with the Whole School, Whole
Community, Whole Child (WSCC) model. The current policy structure addresses comprehensive
health education and student wellness through separate policy documents, which can result in
overlapping policy direction and fragmented implementation. The new approach more
accurately reflects the interconnected conditions that support student health, engagement,
attendance, and academic success.

The proposed revision also establishes a governance and accountability structure not present in
the existing policy framework. The Superintendent would appoint a District Wellness Council to
advise on wellness-related policies, establish roles and timelines through an annual Wellness
Action Plan, assess implementation, and recommend continuous improvement.

Rescission of Board Policy 3.60.060-P is appropriate because the substantive subject matter
addressed by the policy—student wellness through nutrition and physical activity—is
incorporated into the proposed Comprehensive Health & Wellness Policy.

FISCAL IMPACT
The proposed policy itself is not anticipated to create a direct fiscal impact. The policy
establishes a framework to coordinate, implement, monitor, and continuously improve existing
district wellness responsibilities.

Implementation may require staff time and coordination across instructional, operational,
clinical, and administrative departments. The proposed policy does not, by itself, establish new
staffing levels, programs, or funding requirements.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT

Community engagement is an explicit component of the proposed policy. PPS will communicate
opportunities for community participation in developing, implementing, reviewing, and updating
the Wellness Policy and Wellness Action Plan. Community participation in the District Wellness
Council/School Health Advisory Council is one identified engagement mechanism.

TIMELINE FOR IMPLEMENTATION / EVALUATION

Upon Board approval, staff will transition implementation to the Comprehensive Health &
Wellness Policy framework and begin developing and refining the annual Wellness Action Plan.

The District Wellness Council will delineate roles, responsibilities, and actions within the
Wellness Action Plan and assess progress toward implementation annually. The District will
assess and review the policy every three years and update it as needed.

Implementation should include:
   ●​ Establishment or confirmation of the District Wellness Council membership;
   ●​ Development of the annual Wellness Action Plan;
   ●​ Identification of responsible departments and staff for each WSCC component;
   ●​ Establishment of implementation measures and monitoring processes; and
   ●​ Periodic review and reporting on progress and recommended improvements.

CONNECTION TO BOARD GOALS

The proposed policy supports the Board's commitment to equitable student outcomes by
establishing equitable access to health education, wellness supports, nutritious food, physical
activity, safe and supportive environments, and health services.

The policy also supports student academic success by recognizing the relationship between
students' physical and emotional well-being, attendance, engagement, and learning.

The emphasis on continuous improvement, equity, collaboration, and student access provides a
districtwide framework for addressing conditions that support students' ability to thrive.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Board:

    ●​ Approve the revision of Board Policy 6.40.013, Comprehensive Health Education Policy,
       as the Comprehensive Health & Wellness Policy
    ●​ Rescind Board Policy 3.60.060-P, Student Wellness through Nutrition and Physical
       Activity

The proposed action establishes a comprehensive and coordinated policy framework for student
and staff wellness consistent with the Whole School, Whole Community, Whole Child model. It
consolidates related policy provisions, reduces duplication, establishes clearer responsibility for
implementation and evaluation, and provides for an annual Wellness Action Plan and ongoing
review through a District Wellness Council.
