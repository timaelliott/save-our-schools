Overview of the Committee Focus for the Year

Director DePass asked committee members to consider what \the Board and the public should
understand by the end of the school year about the condition, future, and stewardship of PPS facilities.
She suggested the following topics: 2025 bond implementation and capital project delivery; a districtwide
facilities condition and deferred maintenance report and strategy; a seismic safety roadmap; ADA
accessibility and equitable facility conditions; enrollment capacity and the future PPS footprint; a
refreshed long-range facilities plan and potential future bond pipeline; climate resilience, indoor
environmental quality, and operating costs.

Committee members expressed interest in a longer-term conversation about unused buildings and real
estate as the District goes through Rightsizing, including a presentation on the District's full building
portfolio and the history of closed schools over the past 20 years. There was also a request to look at
what other districts, such as Austin, are doing with closed schools. Members also expressed continued
interest in the deferred maintenance report and strategy, seismic safety, and ADA and equitable facility
conditions. Staff noted that all of the topics discussed are already being worked on and agreed to present
on them.

Update: Facility Improvement Wins

Joanna Evenson and TJ Hopkins presented an update on summer facility improvement work:

    ●​ Bond-funded construction: Seismic and roofing projects at seven sites (Woodmere, César
       Chávez, Marshall, Vestal, Vernon, Winterhaven, and Marysville), and facility improvement
       projects at 34 sites which included fire alarms, sidewalks, roof structures, stage rigging,
       classroom technology, science chemical storage, boiler retubes, restroom piping, and cafeteria
       tables.
    ●​ Athletics: This included phase one of Jackson's athletic projects, Marshall's track and field
       lighting, tennis courts, gym, and weight room being completed, and the lights at Grant were
       turned on.
    ●​ Maintenance and custodial: Maintenance crews completed boiler, HVAC, and power conversion
       work at more than 25 sites, playground and athletic repairs at seven sites, ADA ramp rebuilds,
       tree management, window replacements, and other repairs. Schools were deep cleaned.
    ●​ Modernization projects: The RISE design team and project managers completed 50%
       construction document reviews for all three modernization projects. Groundbreaking and
       construction are underway at Jefferson and Cleveland. The Request for Proposal (RFP) for the
       third modernization project's CMGC contractor was published.

There was discussion about green schoolyards and green energy technology. Committee members
expressed appreciation for the summer work completed.

Other Business and Future Agenda Items

Director DePass asked committee members to send topics for the year, and staff offered to present the
topics discussed and to work with members to prioritize. Director Englesman requested a presentation on
the buildings the District owns and the process of disposing of unused real estate.
